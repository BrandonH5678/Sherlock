1. **Target Specification**
   - Research question clearly stated
   - Sources to investigate
   - Deliverable format
   - Success criteria

2. **Autonomous Execution**
   - Web search for peer-reviewed sources
   - Primary source texts when available
   - Skeptical analyses included
   - Multi-perspective gathering

3. **Synthesis Format**
   ```markdown
   # Research Question: [Question]

   **Generated:** [Timestamp]
   **System:** J5A Night Shift Research Synthesis

   ## Findings
   [Comprehensive synthesis with citations]

   ## Gaps Identified
   [What needs deeper investigation]

   ## Source Excerpts
   [Key quotes with citations]

   ## Sources Cited
   [Complete bibliography]
   ```

4. **Quality Standards**
   - Minimum 5 credible sources per question
   - Peer-reviewed sources prioritized
   - Skeptical critiques included
   - Distinguish validated vs speculative claims
   - Research Posture applied (presume validity while testing)
