## Evidence Collection Protocols

### Research Posture Application

**Principle 1:** Treat experiencer phenomenology as primary data
- Sheldrake's observations and experiments = valid data requiring explanation
- Not dismissing due to controversy but engaging with evidence

**Principle 3:** Presume validity until rigorous falsification
- Morphic resonance deserves serious evaluation despite marginalization
- Sheldrake is credentialed biologist (Cambridge PhD, Royal Society fellowship)
- Don't dismiss without engaging actual evidence and arguments

**Epistemic Humility:**
- Mainstream rejection may reflect paradigm protection, not bad science
- Multiple frameworks may be partially valid (morphic + quantum + biological)
- Unknown mechanisms don't invalidate observed phenomena

### Source Credibility Assessment

**Primary Sources (Essential):**
- Sheldrake's own books and papers (primary theory exposition)
- Experimental reports with methodology and data
- Sheldrake's responses to criticisms

**Secondary Sources (Important):**
- Independent experimental replications
- Critical analyses from scientists (not just dismissals)
- Philosophical examinations of theory
- Comparative analyses with other consciousness frameworks

**Tertiary Sources (Contextual):**
- Popular science treatments
- Skeptical critiques (valuable but check for engagement vs dismissal)
- Alternative medicine applications (check for misuse of theory)

**Red Flags:**
- Dismissals without engaging evidence ("pseudoscience" labels without analysis)
- Misuse of morphic resonance for untestable claims
- Cherry-picking positive results while ignoring null findings
