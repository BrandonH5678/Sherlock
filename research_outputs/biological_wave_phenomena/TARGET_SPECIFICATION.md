## Evidence Collection Protocols

### Research Posture Application

**Principle 1:** Treat direct experience as primary data
- Meditation practitioners' phenomenology = valid consciousness wave data
- Flow state experiences = evidence of resonance states
- Not dismissing "subjective" reports but treating as measurements

**Principle 3:** Presume validity until rigorous falsification
- HeartMath research deserves evaluation (not dismissal as "alternative")
- Biofield research requires engagement with actual data
- Mycelial consciousness hypotheses deserve serious consideration

**Epistemic Humility:**
- Neuroscience doesn't fully explain consciousness
- Wave phenomena may be correlates or causal mechanisms (uncertain)
- Multiple frameworks may be partially valid

### Source Credibility Tiers

**Tier 1 (Highest Credibility):**
- Peer-reviewed neuroscience journals (Nature Neuroscience, PNAS)
- Replicated findings on brainwave patterns
- Established research groups (HeartMath needs independent validation)

**Tier 2 (Moderate Credibility):**
- Preliminary studies on biofields, biophotons
- Mycelial network research (emerging field)
- Consciousness studies journals (less mainstream but rigorous)

**Tier 3 (Speculative but Informative):**
- Collective consciousness research (Dean Radin, Global Consciousness Project)
- Biofield interactions and distant healing studies
- Plant consciousness and mycelial intelligence hypotheses

**Red Flags:**
- Claims without measurement (unmeasurable "energy")
- Misuse of quantum terminology for woo
- Biofield therapeutic claims without mechanism or data
