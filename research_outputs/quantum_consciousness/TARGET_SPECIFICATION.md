## Evidence Collection Protocols

### Research Posture Application
**Principle 1:** Treat experiencer/observer reports as primary data
- Consciousness phenomenology (meditation, flow states) = valid data
- Not just "subjective reports" but evidence of consciousness wave states

**Principle 3:** Presume validity until rigorous falsification
- Quantum consciousness theories deserve serious evaluation
- Don't dismiss as "woo" without engaging evidence

**Epistemic Humility:**
- Physics itself is uncertain about consciousness role
- Multiple frameworks may be partially valid
- Gradient truth: Some quantum effects in consciousness likely, full quantum brain uncertain

### Source Credibility Tiers

**Tier 1 (Highest Credibility):**
- Peer-reviewed physics journals (Nature, Science, Physical Review)
- Established physicists (Penrose, Hameroff, Tegmark)
- Experimental studies with replication

**Tier 2 (Moderate Credibility):**
- Conference proceedings, preprints (arXiv)
- Theoretical frameworks without full experimental validation
- Interdisciplinary research (biology-physics-consciousness)

**Tier 3 (Speculative but Informative):**
- Hypothesis papers, philosophical analyses
- Fringe theories with some mathematical rigor
- Historical writings (Tesla, Einstein on consciousness)

**Red Flags (Require Extra Scrutiny):**
- Claims of consciousness creating physical reality without mechanism
- Misuse of quantum terminology for mysticism
- Cherry-picking quantum weirdness without addressing decoherence
