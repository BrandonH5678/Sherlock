## Executive Summary

PKG-WILDCRAFT set out to test whether the Sherlock default model — foraging (opportunistic exploitation of crises by positioned actors) — adequately explains the evidence accumulated across PKG-RESTRUCTURE, PKG-MK, PKG-XFIN, PKG-EP, and PKG-911. Six phases of analysis produced convergent findings:

1. **Phase 1 (Amplification Audit):** 77% of 22 crisis junctures show amplification decisions where stabilization alternatives existed — exceeding the foraging prediction of 40-60%.
2. **Phase 2 (Environmental Maintenance):** 5 of 11 structural conditions score higher on wildcrafting (active maintenance) than foraging (passive persistence); the reform-capture-gutting cycle is the primary maintenance mechanism.
3. **Phase 3 (Pipeline Analysis):** Five credentialing pipelines across four countries share a common architecture: selection gate, bonding mechanism, economic deployment, loyalty enforcement. The breeding model scores highest for China (0.85) and Iran (0.80). The MK-to-SERE lineage documents 70+ years of continuous behavioral science recycling across offensive, defensive, and selection functions.
4. **Phase 4 (Accountability Chronology):** 0/18 reforms achieved lasting structural change. 39% of circumvention mechanisms were SIMULTANEOUS with the reform they neutralized — the strongest wildcrafting indicator. 61% were at-or-before.
5. **Phase 5 (Anti-Fragile Inventory):** 8 of 12 anti-fragile features (67%) score higher on intentional design than natural evolution. Mean design score: 0.61.
6. **Phase 6 (Chaos Audit):** Chaos fraction estimated at 0.25-0.35. The model is not falsified but must be qualified: wildcrafting explains 65-75% of observed dynamics. Strongest in post-crisis institutional design (chaos ~15-20%); weakest in crisis initiation (chaos ~50-60%).

**The campaign's central conclusion:** The evidence does not support pure foraging (opportunistic exploitation) OR pure conspiracy (engineered crises). It supports a **wildcrafting model** — positioned actors who exploit crises they did not start, then actively shape post-crisis architecture, circumvent accountability, maintain crisis-generating conditions, and design anti-fragile infrastructure to preserve extraction capacity across generations. The system is cultivated, not planted. It is tended, not commanded.

### The Phase-Transition Model

The system operates through a repeating cycle:

**Phase 1 — Crisis (Foraging zone, chaos 50-60%):** An exogenous or semi-exogenous crisis occurs. No E1 evidence supports deliberate engineering of any major crisis. Positioned actors exploit immediate opportunities through structural advantage (information asymmetry, cross-faction positions, regulatory arbitrage). This is genuine foraging — the actors did not cause the fire, but they are first to the scene.

**Phase 2 — Aftermath Design (Wildcrafting zone, chaos 15-20%):** The same actors who foraged during the crisis now design the post-crisis architecture. This is where foraging transitions to wildcrafting: Lamont designs the Dawes Plan circular flow. White designs the IMF veto. Dulles preserves the BIS. Chubais implements speed-over-equity privatization. The architects are identifiable, the design decisions are documented at E1, and the stabilization alternatives that were rejected are also documented. The 77% amplification rate applies here.

**Phase 3 — Accountability Avoidance (Wildcrafting zone, chaos 15-20%):** When the crisis aftermath generates exposure or reform pressure, the accountability-avoidance architecture activates. 39% of circumvention mechanisms are SIMULTANEOUS with the reform. 61% are at-or-before. 0/18 reforms achieve lasting structural change. This is not passive resistance to reform — it is active participation in the reform process to ensure it contains its own negation.

**Phase 4 — Environmental Maintenance (Wildcrafting zone, chaos 30-35%):** Between crises, the conditions that generate future crises are maintained. BIS immunities persist. Offshore architecture grows 25% during transparency initiatives. Sanctions enforcement remains at 500:1 ratio. The reform-capture-gutting cycle resets. Turchin PSI variables (elite overproduction, popular immiseration, state fiscal stress) are maintained in all four documented countries despite feasible reform alternatives.

**Phase 5 — Pipeline Reproduction (Breeding zone, chaos 25-35%):** The pipelines that select and credential the next generation of operators continue functioning. S&B taps 15 juniors per year. KA recruits government alumni. FSB Academy trains siloviki children. Seminary graduates intermarry. SERE filters for stress tolerance and moral compartmentalization. The traits selected — loyalty to network over external obligations, crisis exploitation capacity, moral ambiguity tolerance — are the traits that produce Phase 1 foraging and Phase 2 wildcrafting behavior. The system breeds its own operators.

**Cycle repeats.** Each crisis is different. The actors evolve. The institutions adapt. But the cycle — forage → wildcraft → circumvent → maintain → breed → forage — is structurally consistent across 109 years of documented evidence.

### The Spectrum Position

**Aggregate system position on the foraging-wildcrafting spectrum: 0.55-0.65.**

This means the system is more cultivated than opportunistic, but not comprehensively managed. Approximately 65-75% of documented dynamics reflect active cultivation (wildcrafting); 25-35% reflect genuine chaos, incompetence, unintended consequences, and failed cultivation (the chaos fraction from Phase 6). The system is a tended garden with weeds, pests, and weather — not a greenhouse.

## Section 5: Recommendations for Follow-Up Research

### R-1: Test Pipeline Defection Rates (Priority: HIGH)
Phase 3 documented 6 pipeline defectors but could not estimate reliable defection rates due to unknown total pipeline population. A systematic count of S&B members, KA alumni, Safari Club participants, and SERE graduates who subsequently chose stabilization over amplification at documented crisis junctures would test whether the 77% amplification rate holds within specific pipeline outputs or varies by pipeline.

### R-2: Quantify the Accountability Avoidance Architecture (Priority: HIGH)
Phase 4's 0/18 finding needs expansion beyond the sampled episodes. A comprehensive inventory of ALL reform attempts targeting the cross-faction financial infrastructure (not just 18) would test whether the zero success rate holds at scale or is an artifact of episode selection. Specific attention to reforms that partially succeeded (CFPB, Swiss AEOI, BCCI prosecution) to determine whether partial success rates differ by domain.

### R-3: Test Wildcrafting Transfer to Authoritarian Systems (Priority: MEDIUM)
Phase 6 identified that the wildcrafting model degrades when applied to Russia, China, and Iran (fragility, single-point-of-failure, untested succession). PKG-RUS, PKG-CHN, and PKG-IRN phases should explicitly test whether authoritarian wildcrafting operates through different mechanisms than Western market-based wildcrafting — or whether the difference is merely one of institutional maturity.

### R-4: The SERE-to-Corporate Assessment Pipeline (Priority: MEDIUM)
Phase 3 documented the MK-to-SERE lineage but did not fully trace the SERE-to-corporate assessment connection (OSS Station S → AT&T Management Assessment → modern corporate assessment centers). This pipeline — if documented at E1 — would establish that MK-derived behavioral science is now embedded in mainstream corporate personnel selection, not just military/intelligence applications.
