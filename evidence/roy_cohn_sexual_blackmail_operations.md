## 1. THE JAMES ROTHSTEIN TESTIMONY

### Who Is James Rothstein?

**James M. "Boots" Rothstein** — NYPD Police Officer and Detective from **1965 to 1980**. In 1966, assigned to work prostitution and pedophilia cases. His beat: **Eighth Avenue between 42nd and 50th Streets** (the "Minnesota Strip" — at any time, 400+ girls from Minnesota being exploited there). Rothstein was from Minnesota himself.

After a pimp murdered an NYPD officer, Rothstein was sent **undercover** to infiltrate prostitution and pedophilia rings. Spent **12 years undercover**. Awarded **nine medals** (never accepted or displayed them). Repeatedly encountered government agencies "giving him the runaround or right out blocking him from doing his job."

Post-NYPD: Researcher and consultant on human trafficking at national and international levels. Became **Mayor of St. Martin, Minnesota** (since 2002). Continued combating trafficking in rural Minnesota counties.

### The Sit-Down with Cohn

In the **early 1980s**, Rothstein had a "sit-down" with Roy Cohn. Because of Rothstein's position investigating human trafficking for NYPD, he had opportunity to question Cohn directly. **Cohn admitted to being part of an elaborate sexual blackmail operation that compromised politicians with child prostitutes.**

### The Quote

As Rothstein relayed to former Nebraska State Senator **John DeCamp** and confirmed in a **2020 interview with Whitney Webb** (MintPress News):

> **"Cohn's job was to run the little boys. Say you had an admiral, a general, a congressman, who did not want to go along with the program. Cohn's job was to set them up, then they would go along."**

### "The Program"

Cohn told Rothstein this was carried out as **"part of the anti-communist crusade"** — not a freelance criminal enterprise but an activity integrated with government objectives. Uncooperative officials in the military and Congress were sexually compromised to ensure policy compliance during the Cold War.

### Nature of the Source

**NOT sworn testimony before a court or legislature.** It was:
1. A direct conversation between Rothstein (then-NYPD detective) and Cohn
2. Later relayed to **John DeCamp** (former Nebraska state senator investigating the Franklin case)
3. **Confirmed by Rothstein in 2020 interview with Whitney Webb**

Rothstein also indicated that **Cohn himself had been entrapped and blackmailed** — his recruitment into the operation came about because he was a former target. The system perpetuated itself by creating new operators from former victims.

**Evidence grade: E2-E3** (single primary witness but a career law enforcement source with 12 years undercover in exactly this area; confirmed account twice over 40 years; independently corroborated by Klotz and Kaufman)

## 2. THE PLAZA HOTEL OPERATIONS

### Suite 233 ("The Blue Suite")

Operations ran from **Suite 233 at the Plaza Hotel**, Manhattan — described by Susan Kaufman as "a beautiful suite... all done in light blue."

Originally operated from Lewis Rosenstiel's personal home, then relocated to the Plaza.

### Recording Equipment

The suite was **bugged with microphones and recording equipment** that captured activities of high-profile guests. Rosenstiel had previously bugged his own office and applied the same methodology. Attorney John Klotz found evidence of the "blue suite" and its role as a sex extortion ring through **local government documents and private detective investigations.**

Former CIA agent **Frank Terpil** described related methodology in a letter to author Jim Hougan:

> **"Historically, one of Wilson's Agency jobs was to subvert members of both houses [of Congress] by any means necessary... Certain people could be easily coerced by living out their sexual fantasies in the flesh... A remembrance of these occasions [was] permanently recorded via selected cameras... The technicians in charge of filming were TSD [Technical Services Division of the CIA]."**

Note: Terpil's statement references Edwin Wilson's operations specifically, not Cohn's Plaza operations directly. But it documents the same methodology at the CIA institutional level.

### Operational Pattern

1. Targets (military officers, politicians, congressmen) invited to social events
2. **Minors** ("little boys") present and made available
3. Activities **recorded** via hidden equipment
4. Resulting material used for **coercion/blackmail** to ensure cooperation with "the program"

### Duration

At least **late 1950s** (Kaufman's 1958 testimony) through **mid-1980s** (Cohn's death 1986). The broader blackmail infrastructure predated Cohn's involvement, originating with Rosenstiel in the early 1950s.

## 3. SUSAN KAUFMAN ROSENSTIEL TESTIMONY

### Who Is Susan Kaufman?

**Fourth wife of Lewis S. Rosenstiel**, chairman of Schenley Industries (major liquor company). Marriage ended in bitter divorce.

### What She Testified

Described a **1958 party at Plaza Hotel, Suite 233** hosted by Roy Cohn:

- Cohn introduced her to **J. Edgar Hoover, who was in drag**
- Cohn told her Hoover's name was **"Mary"** in a fit of barely concealed laughter
- Hoover wore **"a fluffy black dress, very fluffy, with flounces and lace stockings and high heels, and a black curly wig"** with makeup and false eyelashes
- **Young boys were present**
- She claimed Cohn, Hoover, and Rosenstiel engaged in sexual activity with minors
- She heard Rosenstiel say: **"If Hoover ever brings pressure against Lansky or any of us, we'll use this as blackmail"**

### When and To Whom

Testimony before the **New York Joint Legislative Committee on Crime** (1970s). Later provided information to **Anthony Summers** for his 1993 book *Official and Confidential: The Secret Life of J. Edgar Hoover*. Signed affidavit asserting truthfulness.

### Credibility Assessment — CONTESTED

**For:**
- **Judge Edward McLaughlin** (Chief Counsel, Crime Committee): "I thought her absolutely truthful"
- Committee investigator **William Gallinaro**: "an excellent witness"
- Summers conducted **six years of repeated interviews** — account remained consistent
- Reportedly **corroborated by two separate witnesses**

**Against:**
- **Convicted of perjury in 1971** — served time at Rikers Island (though Crime Committee interviewees told Summers the charge was "unprecedented and bizarre" and believed **instigated by Rosenstiel to discredit her**)
- Possible **revenge motive** against Hoover (FBI agents surveilled her during divorce)
- Summers **paid her** for the story
- Journalist **Peter Maas**: after the alleged incident, Hoover actually assigned agents to investigate Lansky — which undermines the blackmail theory

**Evidence grade: E3** (single primary witness with perjury conviction; however, found credible by Crime Committee officials; broader blackmail operation corroborated by other sources)

## 4. THE ROSENSTIEL-COHN COMMAND STRUCTURE

### LIFE Magazine (September 5, 1969) — "The Hotshot One-Man Roy Cohn Lobby"

Cohn and Rosenstiel had for years referred to one another as:
- **"Field Commander"** — Cohn
- **"Supreme Commander"** — Rosenstiel

Meyer Lansky also called Rosenstiel "Supreme Commander" — positioning Rosenstiel within Lansky's organized crime hierarchy, not merely as an independent businessman.

### Lewis Rosenstiel Profile

Multimillionaire boss of the Schenley distillery complex. Long-time Meyer Lansky associate. LIFE documented: "Cohn's friendship with Director Hoover was cemented by their mutual regard for Lewis Rosenstiel."

### Chain of Command Implications

The naming conventions suggest:
- **Lansky** → strategic direction (also called Rosenstiel "Supreme Commander")
- **Rosenstiel** → operational command ("Supreme Commander")
- **Cohn** → field execution ("Field Commander")

This implies **organized crime provided the infrastructure and tradecraft** while **intelligence/government interests provided the targeting priorities and protection.** The most likely structure: a convergence of Mafia operational capability with intelligence community objectives.

## 5. THE CRAIG SPENCE RING

### Craig Spence's Operation

**Craig J. Spence** — American journalist, lobbyist, socialite in Washington DC (1980s):
- Spent **$20,000+/month** on male prostitutes from a DC ring
- Threw lavish parties for senior government officials, military officers, journalists, politicians
- House was **bugged with recording equipment and a two-way mirror**
- Offered cocaine alongside sexual services
- **Bragged of CIA connections** — claimed CIA used the call-boy service to compromise federal intelligence officials and foreign diplomats

### Washington Times Exposé (June 29, 1989)

**Front-page headline:** "Homosexual Prostitution Inquiry Ensnares VIPs With Reagan, Bush: 'Call Boys' Took Tour of the White House"

Named clients: **Charles K. Dutcher** (Reagan administration associate director of presidential personnel), **Paul R. Balach** (Labor Secretary Dole's liaison). Intelligence authorities feared **"a nest of homosexuals at top levels of the Reagan administration may have been penetrated by Soviet-backed espionage agents posing as male prostitutes."**

### White House Midnight Tours

Spence arranged **at least four midnight tours of the White House**, including one on June 29, 1988, on which he took a **15-year-old boy** (falsely identified as his son). Two male prostitutes were among guests. Guard **Reginald deGueldre** facilitated access.

### Spence-Cohn Connection
- Both CIA Director Casey and Roy Cohn were reportedly close with Spence
- Spence **hosted a birthday party for Cohn** — Casey attended
- Spence frequently bragged about his association with Cohn
- Washington Times reports revealed **"close ties to Roy Cohn"**

### Spence's Death (November 10, 1989)

Found dead in **Room 429, Boston Ritz-Carlton**:
- Door **barricaded** by bed; firefighters sawed door in half to enter
- Dressed in **black tuxedo**, white bow tie and suspenders, $3 in pocket
- Set out **his will and birth certificate**
- Previously diagnosed with AIDS
- Previously told friends he worried **CIA might kill him and "make it look like a suicide"**
- Scrawled on the mirror: **"Chief, consider this my resignation, effective immediately. As you always said, you can't ask for a miracle but one. Ask for another and you're pressing your luck. I'm not pressing my luck. — CJS"**
- Newspaper clipping about CIA Director Webster protecting agents from testimony lay next to him on the bed

## 6. THE FRANKLIN SCANDAL CONNECTION

### Lawrence King Jr.'s Operation

**Lawrence E. King Jr.** ran the Franklin Community Federal Credit Union, Omaha, Nebraska. June 1988: allegations that King hosted "lavish parties at which minors were sexually abused." Children recruited from **foster homes, Boys Town orphanage**, flown to Washington DC for exploitation by politicians, businessmen, and law enforcement.

### The Spence-King Link

Larry King's name found in **credit card records from Spence's prostitution ring**. Per Nick Bryant: the Franklin network had **two primary pimps: Larry King in Nebraska** (procuring from institutions) and **Craig Spence in Washington, D.C.**

### Cohn's Connection to Franklin

The link is **indirect but structural:**
1. **Rothstein told DeCamp** about Cohn's admissions
2. **Craig Spence** was documented as connected to both Cohn AND the Franklin network via King
3. Identical operational methodology (sexual blackmail of politicians using minors, intelligence involvement)
4. **Robert Keith Gray** (Hill & Knowlton CEO) — described by DeCamp as "a CIA specialist in homosexual blackmail operations" who had "collaborated with Roy Cohn." Gray served on the board of **Consultants International**, an Edwin Wilson-founded CIA front company

### Paul Bonacci

Claimed to be a victim. Alleged transport from Omaha to Washington DC. Federal judge found claims credible — awarded **$1M default judgment** against King. **No direct testimony from Bonacci naming Cohn** found — the connection runs through Rothstein-DeCamp chain.

### Gary Caradori — Investigator Death

**Gary Caradori** — chief investigator for Nebraska legislature's Franklin Committee. Called boss State Senator **Loran Schmit** (July 10, 1990): "We now have the subjects by the short hairs."

**Next morning (July 11, 1990, 2:40-2:57 AM):** His plane **disintegrated in mid-air** near Ashton, Illinois. **His 8-year-old son also died.** NTSB: wings snapped off. Personal effects returned to widow — **but not the briefcase containing investigation results** (went missing). Senator Schmit submitted affidavit he had been warned Caradori's life was in danger.

Official finding (separate state and federal grand juries, late 1990): allegations were **"unfounded"** and a **"carefully crafted hoax."**

## 8. INTELLIGENCE CONTEXT — SEXUAL BLACKMAIL AS DOCUMENTED TRADECRAFT

### Operation Midnight Climax (CIA/MKUltra, 1953-1963)

Established by **Sidney Gottlieb**, run by FBN operative **George Hunter White**. CIA safehouses in San Francisco and NYC. Prostitutes on CIA payroll lured men, dosed them with LSD, monitored behind **one-way glass**. CIA analyzed optimal timing for information extraction — concluded it was **immediately after sex**.

**Significance:** Proves CIA was institutionally willing to combine sexual activity with surveillance for intelligence purposes — direct methodological precedent. [E1: Church Committee documentation]

- **Joseph Alsop (1957):** Journalist lured into gay honeytrap in Moscow. Photographed. Refused to cooperate, reported to U.S. authorities
- **John Vassall (1954):** British civil servant photographed in homosexual encounters. Blackmailed into spying until 1962 arrest. 18-year sentence
- **Jeremy Wolfenden (1960s):** British journalist/MI6 asset. Hidden cameras. Blackmailed into double-agent work. Died at 31 from alcohol

### The Profumo Affair (1963, Britain)
War Secretary John Profumo shared mistress (Christine Keeler, age 19) with Soviet naval attaché. MI5 may have had foreknowledge. Profumo forced to resign for lying to Parliament; helped bring down Macmillan government.

### The Terpil/Wilson CIA Operations
Frank Terpil documented that **Edwin Wilson's CIA-sanctioned operations** specifically used sexual compromise to subvert members of Congress, with **CIA Technical Services Division providing recording technology**. This is the closest documented CIA parallel to what Rothstein describes Cohn operating.
