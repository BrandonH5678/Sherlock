**The most significant original finding of Phase 4 is a direct primary source confirmation that Bear Stearns was named in the 1991 Price Waterhouse Sandstorm Report as one of the main brokers used by BCCI's Treasury division.** This is not a secondary claim or a journalist's inference — it is in the OCR text of the Sandstorm report itself, available at `/home/johnny5/Sherlock/evidence/sandstorm_part2_ocr.txt`, Section 4, listing BCCI Treasury brokers: "Refco / Capital Commodity Dealers Ltd ('Capcom') / Rudolf Wolff / Bear Steans [sic]." The name is misspelled in the OCR as "Bear Steans" due to scanning artifacts but the identification is unambiguous in context.

Rudolf Wolff is then called out specifically for allowing Sandstorm a $40 million overdrawn balance "possibly used to conceal losses/'park'" positions. Bear Stearns is listed as a named broker without the specific aggravating detail that applies to Wolff and Capcom — meaning the report documents Bear Stearns as a Treasury broker but does not specifically allege that Bear Stearns facilitated manipulation. The distinction matters for evidence grading.

Beyond this core BCCI finding, Phase 4 examines the intelligence implications of Bear Stearns' primary dealer status, the CIA-Wall Street institutional relationship, the Liquid Funding Ltd. mystery (particularly the 60% unknown ownership), and the structural case for and against a Greenberg-as-CIA-asset hypothesis.
