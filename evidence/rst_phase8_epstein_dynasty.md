**The correct analytical frame is not "Epstein dynasty" but "Epstein as recruited operator within Maxwell dynasty infrastructure."** The sexual blackmail function itself predates both Epstein and Maxwell — documented predecessors include Lewis Rosenstiel/Roy Cohn (1950s-1980s) and CIA Operation Midnight Climax (1954-1965). Jeffrey Edward Epstein was born January 20, 1953, in Brooklyn, New York. His parents were:

The family lived in **Sea Gate**, a private gated community at the western tip of Coney Island. Jeffrey had one sibling: **Mark Epstein** (born 1954). Both parents were children of immigrants from Eastern Europe. Much of the extended family perished in the Holocaust.

There is **zero evidence** of:
