**Fred Collins v. Straight, Inc. (1982-1984):**
In 1982, 19-year-old Fred Collins Jr. visited his brother at Straight Inc.'s facility near St. Petersburg, Florida. Staff detained him against his will for approximately 10 months. In 1990, a jury awarded Karen Norton $721,000 in damages. Norton, a patient in Straight's Florida facility in 1982, alleged that staff members assaulted her, denied her health care, and refused to give her permission to visit her dying grandfather. [E1, 0.95 — court record]

A Straight Inc. spin-off called "Kids of North Jersey," founded by former Straight national clinical director Dr. V. Miller Newton, settled an abuse claim for $4.5 million. [E1, 0.90]
