## 2. ORIGIN STORY: CHARLES DEDERICH AND THE LSD SESSION

### 2.1 Dederich Biography

Charles Edwin Dederich Sr. was born March 22, 1913 in Toledo, Ohio. His father, an alcoholic, was killed in an automobile accident when Dederich was four years old. He found work as a traveling salesman for Gulf Oil's Toledo office, married, and had one son. His alcohol dependency deepened over decades. [E1, 0.85]

By age 43, Dederich had a "dead career" and two failed marriages. He relocated to Santa Monica and, after three years of continued decline, walked into his first Alcoholics Anonymous meeting. He worked through the Twelve-Step Program, and while "wary of AA's spirituality," was "enamored of its family-like dynamic of mutual support." **CRITICAL FACT:** Dederich had no therapy background, no college degree, and no formal training in psychology or medicine. [E1, 0.95]

## 3. THE GAME — METHODOLOGY AND RULES

### 3.1 Core Structure

"The Game" (also called "The Synanon Game") was the cornerstone of Synanon's therapeutic and later coercive methodology. It was a form of attack therapy — a group session in which one member would speak about themselves and then endure intense, sustained criticism by their peers. [E1, 0.95 — extensively documented]

**Session Format:**
- Standard sessions ran approximately 2-3 hours in the early period
- Extended sessions ("marathons") lasted up to 40 hours with only a 4-hour break for sleep
- The 72-hour version was the most extreme, acknowledged by Dederich himself as brainwashing [E2, 0.80]
- Groups typically consisted of 10-15 participants
- Sessions were held multiple times per week, with participation mandatory [E1, 0.90]

### 3.2 Rules and Methodology

**The Attack Phase:**
- Members were encouraged to use harsh, profane language without restriction
- The target was subjected to "verbal haircuts" — sustained, no-holds-barred attacks on personal pretenses, character flaws, and vulnerabilities
- The group would collectively identify the target's most vulnerable psychological points and concentrate attacks there
- Interrupting and jeering were standard; screaming and yelling were common
- Physical violence was prohibited (at least initially), but psychological violence was unlimited [E1, 0.90]

**Participation Requirements:**
- All members were required to participate actively in attacks
- Refusal to attack other members resulted in punishment
- The target was expected to accept all criticism without defensive responses
- Empathy and validation were explicitly prohibited during sessions [E1, 0.85]

**Ego Destruction Goal:**
- The explicit methodology was to "tear them down in order to build them up"
- The goal was destruction of the individual ego so the person could be "educated in the inherent thought-patterns of the group and the group leader"
- Sessions were designed to "induce profound anxiety to compel behavioral overhaul" [E1, 0.90]

### 3.3 Entry Requirements and Isolation

- New members were required to quit all drugs and alcohol immediately ("cold turkey") upon entry
- No medications — including medically prescribed ones — were permitted
- No contact or communication with the outside world was allowed for the first 90 days
- Members were geographically isolated at Synanon properties
- Access to transportation was restricted [E1, 0.90]

### 3.4 Dederich's Own Assessment

Dederich made explicit statements acknowledging the brainwashing nature of his methods:

> "Brainwashing is a very apt term. We get very dirty brains."

He "later admitted to realizing that he was brainwashing his program members." [E1, 0.95 — direct quote, multiple sources]
