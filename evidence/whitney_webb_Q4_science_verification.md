This report evaluates Whitney Webb's claims regarding Jeffrey Epstein's science and technology network as presented in "One Nation Under Blackmail" (2022) and subsequent Unlimited Hangout reporting, cross-referencing them against independently verifiable records including the 2026 DOJ document release (3+ million pages), Bloomberg's authenticated email cache (18,700 emails, 2002-2022), Harvard and MIT institutional investigations, IRS 990 filings, and mainstream investigative reporting.

**Key Findings:**

The factual substrate of Webb's science/technology claims is substantially verified. Epstein's funding of Harvard PED ($6.5M+), MIT Media Lab (post-conviction donations routed through intermediaries), Edge Foundation ($638K via IRS 990 filings), George Church genomics research ($686K), and the 2002 AI symposium on Little Saint James are all documented through primary sources. These overarching claims remain in E2-E3 territory -- plausible given the pattern of evidence but not directly documented by the released files.

**Overall Verification Assessment: MODERATE (0.55-0.65)**
Individual factual claims range from E1 (0.90+) for documented funding to E3 (0.20-0.35) for the intelligence-coordination thesis. The gap between documented facts and Webb's interpretive framework is the critical analytical divide.
