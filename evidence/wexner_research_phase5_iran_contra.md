## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report maps the intersection of Iran-Contra operations, Bear Stearns financial networks, and Leslie Wexner's Columbus, Ohio business empire through the figure of Jeffrey Epstein. The research reveals a documented infrastructure continuity where Southern Air Transport (SAT) -- a former CIA proprietary airline used in Iran-Contra arms flights -- relocated to Wexner's logistics base at Rickenbacker Airport in Columbus with substantial state subsidies, while Epstein served as Wexner's financial agent with full power of attorney. The report also traces Epstein's opaque 1981-1986 "gap years" between leaving Bear Stearns and becoming Wexner's financial adviser, a period in which multiple sources place him in the orbit of arms dealers Douglas Leese and Adnan Khashoggi. The introduction chain connecting Bear Stearns chairman Ace Greenberg, insurance executive Robert Meister, and Wexner is reconstructed with conflicting accounts noted.

**Overall Assessment:** The infrastructure continuity (SAT to Rickenbacker) is factually documented at E1-E2 levels. The thesis that this represented a deliberate intelligence-linked transition rather than a commercial coincidence rests primarily on Hoffenberg's claims (a convicted fraudster) and investigative journalism, placing the intentionality claim at E2-E3. The Greenberg-Cohn-Epstein-Wexner network is documented at multiple nodes but the claim of a coordinated introduction scheme remains inferential.
