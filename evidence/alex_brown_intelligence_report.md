## CRITICAL FINDINGS -- TOP 8

### Finding 1: DBAB Executed Anomalous UAL Put Options September 6-7, 2001 (E2)

Deutsche Bank Alex. Brown was identified as the executing broker for a significant portion of the anomalous pre-9/11 put options, specifically the 4,744 UAL put option contracts purchased September 6-7, 2001, reflecting a 12:1 put/call ratio against a normal baseline of approximately 1:1. The firm processed these trades through the Chicago Board Options Exchange. The 9/11 Commission attributed 95% of these UAL puts to "a single U.S.-based institutional investor with no conceivable ties to al Qaeda."

**Evidence tier: E2 (The Independent, San Francisco Chronicle, Foreign Policy Journal, multiple corroborating sources)**
**Significance:** DBAB served as the executing broker -- the institution through which purchase orders were routed and processed. This does not prove the firm itself had foreknowledge, but the institutional provenance is analytically significant given Krongard's leadership history.

### Finding 2: Peer-Reviewed Statistical Analyses Confirm Informed Trading (E1)

Two independent peer-reviewed academic studies using different methodologies both concluded the pre-9/11 options trading was consistent with informed trading at confidence levels exceeding 95%:

| Study | Finding | Journal |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Poteshman (2006) | "99% probability" AMR options were informed trading | Journal of Business (U of Chicago) |
| Chesney/Crameri/Mancini (2011/2015) | $15M+ in informed gains across 6+ companies (UAL, AMR, Morgan Stanley, Merrill Lynch, BoA, Citigroup, JPMorgan) | Journal of Empirical Finance |

These studies directly contradict the 9/11 Commission's "innocuous" characterization. The academic consensus (0.85 confidence that informed trading occurred) exists alongside the official finding of no foreknowledge.

**Evidence tier: E1 (peer-reviewed publications in top-tier finance journals)**

### Finding 3: Mayo Shattuck III Resigned September 12, 2001 -- No Explanation (E2)

Krongard's hand-picked successor as Chairman of DBAB resigned abruptly on September 12, 2001 -- the first business day after the attacks -- despite holding a three-year contract worth several million dollars annually. Shattuck subsequently became CEO of Constellation Energy (Baltimore utility) and has declined to comment on the resignation timing.

**Evidence tier: E2 (Baltimore Sun, financial press)**
**Significance:** The head of the institution that processed the pre-9/11 put options walked away from a multi-million-dollar contract one day after the attacks. Confidence that the timing is coincidental: 0.40. Confidence that it reflects non-public information: 0.35. Unresolvable without further evidence: 0.25.

### Finding 6: Alex. Brown-Brown Brothers Harriman Genealogical Connection (E1/E3)

The Brown family financial dynasty produced both Alex. Brown & Sons (Baltimore) and Brown Brothers (New York), which merged with W. Averell Harriman's firm to form Brown Brothers Harriman (BBH) in 1931. Prescott Bush was a BBH partner. This means the Brown financial dynasty connects to both the pre-9/11 put options story (through Alex. Brown --> Deutsche Bank) and the Bush-Harriman-intelligence complex (through BBH). This genealogical connection is established at E1 but operational institutional links in the modern era remain E3.

**Evidence tier: E1 (genealogy); E3 (modern operational links)**

### Finding 7: IOSCO Characterized the Investigation as the "Most Important Crime of Insider Trading Ever Committed" (E2)

The International Organisation of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) reportedly characterized the multi-country investigation as relating to "the most important crime of insider trading ever committed." Investigations were launched in Belgium, Cyprus, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Luxembourg, Monaco, Netherlands, Switzerland, and the United States. Despite this characterization, the investigation produced zero indictments.

**Evidence tier: E2 (multiple journalistic sources citing IOSCO)**

### Finding 8: The "Alex. Brown" Name Was Retired One Month After 9/11 (E2)

Deutsche Bank ceased using the Alex. Brown name entirely in October 2001 -- one month after the September 11 attacks. Deutsche Bank stated this was part of a planned global rebranding. The timing coincidence has been noted by researchers, though no causal connection to the put options controversy has been established.

**Evidence tier: E2 (Baltimore Sun, October 4, 2001)**
