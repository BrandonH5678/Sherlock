## Executive Summary

Phases 1-6 of PKG-WILDCRAFT established a foraging-wildcrafting hybrid as the best-fit model for elite behavior during crises: 77% amplification rate (P1), 5/11 conditions actively maintained (P2), 5 parallel credentialing pipelines with common architecture (P3), 0/18 reforms achieving lasting change with 39% simultaneous circumvention (P4), 8/12 anti-fragile features intentionally designed (P5), and a chaos fraction of 0.25-0.35 (P6). The evidence base is strong but the analytical framework has not yet been tested FORWARD — all findings are retrospective pattern identification.

This phase generates testable predictions for four models of elite behavior during systemic disruptions, each producing distinguishable observable outcomes by 2030. The four models are:

- **Model A (Opportunistic):** Actors react to events — no pre-positioning, no cultivation. Crises are exogenous; exploitation is structural.
- **Model B (Cultivation):** Actors shape conditions and aftermath — they do not cause events but tend the results. This is the foraging-wildcrafting hybrid identified in Phases 1-6.
- **Model C (Engineering):** Actors create events directly — crises are planned and executed by beneficiaries.
- **Model D (Selection):** Institutional pipelines produce leaders who naturally create favorable conditions through trained instinct rather than conscious cultivation. The pipeline IS the strategy; individual intent is secondary.

**Critical note on Model B:** This is the model best supported by the PKG-WILDCRAFT evidence base (chaos fraction 0.25-0.35, amplification at 77%, crisis engineering at E1 = 0.00). The predictions below are designed to FURTHER TEST this model against the alternatives, not to confirm it. Each prediction is constructed to distinguish one model from the other three.

## Model A: Opportunistic (No Pre-Positioning)

### Core Thesis
Actors exploit crises they did not anticipate. Structural position provides windfall gains. The 77% amplification rate reflects rational exploitation of available options, not cultivation. The reform failure rate reflects normal democratic difficulty, not designed circumvention. Anti-fragile features are emergent market adaptations.

### Confirming Predictions (testable by 2030)

**A-CONFIRM-1: Next major crisis will show NO pre-positioned beneficiaries in the aftermath architecture.**
If the next systemic disruption (AI displacement crisis, sovereign debt crisis, climate catastrophe, pandemic) produces a post-crisis architecture where the architects have NO prior structural relationship to the crisis domain, Model A is confirmed. Specifically: the post-crisis institutional designers should be domain experts and civil servants, not revolving-door figures from the industries that contributed to the crisis.
- **Distinguishes from B/C/D:** Models B, C, and D all predict that aftermath architects will have pre-existing structural relationships to the crisis domain. Model A alone predicts that exploitation is ad hoc — whoever is closest exploits, without prior positioning.
- **Test:** Track the next major post-crisis institutional design process (likely AI regulation or sovereign debt restructuring). Map the architects' prior career networks to the crisis domain. If <30% have prior structural relationships, Model A is supported.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (biographical records, appointment histories)

**A-CONFIRM-2: Reform success rate will improve to 30%+ in the 2026-2030 period.**
If Model A is correct (exploitation is opportunistic, not systematically maintained), then reforms should succeed at a non-trivial rate when political conditions are favorable. The 0/18 historical rate reflects episodic political failure, not structural accountability avoidance. Model A predicts that at least 2 of the next 6-8 major financial/governance reforms will achieve lasting structural change — because there is no systematic circumvention mechanism.
- **Distinguishes from B/C/D:** Model B predicts continued 0% lasting reform success (circumvention is structural). Model C predicts reforms are irrelevant (controllers determine outcomes). Model D predicts reforms will be captured by pipeline graduates in implementation.
- **Test:** Track outcomes of: EU AI Act implementation, US beneficial ownership re-reform, BRICS payment system regulation, Basel III endgame, digital asset regulation, and any post-crisis legislation. Score each for lasting structural change vs. capture/gutting by 2030.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (legislative and regulatory records)

**A-CONFIRM-3: Anti-fragile features will fail under sufficient stress — system will NOT adapt to a novel enforcement paradigm.**
Model A predicts that the 8/12 intentionally designed anti-fragile features (P5) will break under a sufficiently novel regulatory approach because they are not maintained by conscious design but by market inertia. A genuinely novel enforcement method (e.g., AI-driven transaction monitoring, real-time UBO verification, blockchain-enforced sanctions compliance) will reduce evasion by 50%+ within 3 years of deployment.
- **Distinguishes from B/C/D:** Models B and D predict adaptation — cultivators or pipeline graduates will capture or circumvent the new tool. Model C predicts the tool will never be deployed if it threatens controllers. Only Model A predicts that sufficient enforcement innovation can genuinely disrupt the system.
- **Test:** Track any AI-powered enforcement deployment (FinCEN AI monitoring, EU AMLA operations, FATF technology standards). Measure evasion reduction vs. adaptation rate.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (enforcement statistics, evasion metrics)

### Falsifying Predictions

**A-FALSIFY-1: If the next crisis produces post-crisis architects drawn from the same 50-100 individuals/institutions documented in PKG-RESTRUCTURE and PKG-XFIN evidence base, Model A is falsified.**
Model A requires that exploitation is ad hoc. If the same network nodes (S&C, Goldman Sachs, KA alumni, Big Three asset managers, BIS-affiliated central bankers) dominate post-crisis architecture design in the next systemic disruption, the "coincidence" of persistent structural positioning falsifies opportunism. The test is not whether these actors participate (they are large institutions) but whether they disproportionately shape the architecture relative to their size.
- **Test:** Compare post-crisis architect network overlap with PKG-RESTRUCTURE/XFIN entities. If >60% overlap, Model A is falsified.

**A-FALSIFY-2: If the next reform-circumvention episode shows SIMULTANEOUS timing (bypass embedded during design), Model A is falsified.**
Model A predicts reactive circumvention (AFTER timing). If the next major reform is circumvented through mechanisms embedded during its own design process — as with the oil price cap attestation (WC-062), Bush Mockingbird reform (WC-058), or Dodd-Frank lobbying (P4 #14) — then the reform process itself is being shaped by cultivators, not merely reacted to by opportunists.
- **Test:** Analyze timing of circumvention mechanisms in any major reform enacted 2026-2030.

## Model B: Cultivation (Foraging-Wildcrafting Hybrid)

### Core Thesis
Actors do not cause crises but actively shape their aftermath, maintain crisis-generating conditions, and circumvent reforms that would constrain future exploitation. The system forages during crises (opportunistic exploitation) and wildcrafts during the aftermath (active cultivation). Chaos fraction of 0.25-0.35 reflects genuine incompetence, unintended consequences, and failed cultivation. This is the model best supported by Phases 1-6.

### Confirming Predictions (testable by 2030)

**B-CONFIRM-1: The CTA (Corporate Transparency Act) gutting will be followed by a new transparency reform within 5 years that is ALSO captured or gutted — completing a second full reform-capture-gutting cycle on the same issue.**
The reform-capture-gutting cycle (WC-023, 7 documented instances) predicts that public pressure from the next financial scandal will produce a replacement transparency measure, which will then be weakened through the same mechanisms. The cycle is self-sustaining: gutting produces the opacity that generates the next scandal that produces the next reform.
- **Distinguishes from A/C/D:** Model A predicts that a replacement reform may succeed (no systematic circumvention). Model C predicts no meaningful reform will be attempted. Model D predicts pipeline graduates will implement reform in a form that preserves pipeline interests, but the mechanism will be personnel capture rather than the reform-capture-gutting cycle documented in P4.
- **Test:** Track any successor to CTA or beneficial ownership reform 2026-2030. Score for capture/gutting vs. lasting implementation.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (legislative records, lobbying disclosures)

**B-CONFIRM-2: SWIFT alternatives (CIPS, mBridge, BRICS Bridge) will reach 15-25% of cross-border settlement volume by 2030, but will NOT replace SWIFT — producing a dual-track system that increases intermediary arbitrage opportunities.**
The wildcrafting model predicts that the erosion of SWIFT is real but managed: the system adapts to produce MORE complexity, not less. A dual-track settlement architecture creates new arbitrage opportunities for the same intermediary actors (energy traders, offshore banks, correspondent banking networks) that profit from the current sanctions arbitrage. Total intermediary profits from cross-border payment arbitrage will increase, not decrease.
- **Distinguishes from A/C/D:** Model A predicts SWIFT alternatives grow based on genuine market demand with no particular beneficiary pattern. Model C predicts the outcome is engineered by a specific actor (China or US). Model D predicts pipeline graduates will end up managing both systems, but the dual-track outcome itself is structural, not cultivated.
- **Test:** Track CIPS/mBridge/BRICS Bridge transaction volumes. Track intermediary bank profits from cross-system settlement. If dual-track emerges AND intermediary profits increase, Model B is confirmed.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (central bank data, corporate filings)

**B-CONFIRM-3: The next post-crisis institutional design process will show the same temporal pattern: foraging during the crisis (6-18 months of genuine chaos and opportunistic exploitation) followed by wildcrafting during the aftermath (architects with pre-existing structural relationships designing extractive architecture).**
The foraging-to-wildcrafting transition (WC-007, WC-013) was documented across Versailles/Dawes Plan, Bretton Woods, CIA Act, post-Soviet privatization. The model predicts this temporal signature will repeat in the next major crisis: genuine chaos in the acute phase, then cultivated architecture in the resolution phase.
- **Distinguishes from A/C/D:** Model A predicts no temporal transition — exploitation is consistent throughout. Model C predicts control during the crisis itself (no genuine chaos phase). Model D predicts the transition is automatic (pipeline graduates default to cultivating outcomes) rather than consciously chosen.
- **Test:** In the next major crisis, score the first 12 months for genuine chaos indicators (positioned actors suffering losses, contradictory elite responses, failed coordination) vs. the resolution phase (convergent architecture, pre-positioned architects, familiar institutional designs). If the temporal signature matches (chaos-then-cultivation), Model B is confirmed.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E2 (real-time observation, financial records)

### Falsifying Predictions

**B-FALSIFY-1: If a major reform achieves lasting structural change AND the crisis condition it addressed does not re-emerge through a different mechanism within 5 years, Model B is weakened.**
Model B predicts that the reform-capture-gutting cycle is self-sustaining (0/18 lasting success). A genuine, lasting reform success — where the reformed condition stays reformed and does not simply migrate to a new institutional vehicle — would demonstrate that the system is not cultivated but merely resilient. The reform must address a CORE condition (not a peripheral node like BCCI).
- **Test:** Track EU AI Act, Basel III endgame, or any major financial reform for genuine lasting impact through 2030.

**B-FALSIFY-2: If the chaos fraction in the next crisis exceeds 0.50 — meaning more than half the dynamics are genuinely chaotic, incompetent, or contrary to elite interests — the wildcrafting component of Model B is falsified.**
Model B predicts a chaos fraction of 0.25-0.35 concentrated in crisis initiation. If the next crisis shows cultivators failing MORE than succeeding (>50% chaos), the model overstates cultivation capacity. Specifically: if post-crisis architecture is contested, fragmented, and produces outcomes that positioned actors did not anticipate or benefit from, wildcrafting is falsified even if foraging continues.
- **Test:** Score next major crisis using the Phase 1 amplification methodology. Count amplification vs. non-amplification junctures.

## Model C: Engineering (Direct Crisis Creation)

### Core Thesis
Actors create crises directly to profit from them. The 77% amplification rate understates reality — amplification occurs at ALL junctures including initiation. The 0/18 reform rate reflects total system control. The chaos fraction is near zero; apparent chaos is cover. This is the "conspiracy" model.

### Confirming Predictions (testable by 2030)

**C-CONFIRM-1: E1 evidence of deliberate crisis engineering will emerge for at least one major crisis (2008 financial crisis, COVID-19, or a future event).**
Phases 1-6 found NO E1 evidence for deliberate crisis engineering across all crises examined (WC-094). Model C predicts this is because evidence has been concealed, not because engineering did not occur. If declassification, whistleblower testimony, or investigative journalism produces E1 evidence that a major crisis was deliberately triggered by actors who profited from it, Model C is confirmed.
- **Distinguishes from A/B/D:** Models A, B, and D all predict that NO E1 crisis engineering evidence will emerge because engineering did not occur. Only Model C predicts that evidence exists but is concealed.
- **Test:** Monitor declassification releases, FOIA responses, whistleblower disclosures, and investigative journalism for evidence of deliberate crisis creation with profit motive.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (documentary evidence of deliberate engineering)

**C-CONFIRM-2: Pre-positioning evidence (financial positions taken BEFORE a crisis by actors who subsequently profited from the aftermath) will be documented at E1 for at least one future crisis.**
Model C predicts that crisis engineers position themselves financially before triggering events. Analogous to the PKG-911 put options investigation (which found exploitation but not conclusive foreknowledge at E1), future crises should show a pattern of pre-crisis financial positioning by actors who subsequently shape the aftermath.
- **Distinguishes from A/B/D:** Model A predicts no pre-positioning (exploitation is reactive). Model B predicts structural positioning (permanent advantageous position) but not crisis-specific financial positioning. Model D predicts pipeline graduates will be in advantageous positions by default, not through crisis-specific preparation.
- **Test:** Track unusual financial activity (options, credit default swaps, currency positions) in the 30-90 days before any major crisis. Compare to post-crisis beneficiary mapping.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1 (financial records, trading data)

**C-CONFIRM-3: The Helms-style evidence destruction pattern (WC-059) will repeat — records relevant to a current or future crisis will be destroyed by actors within the institutions under investigation, BEFORE the investigation reaches the relevant records.**
Model C predicts that evidence destruction is systematic and pre-emptive because there IS evidence of engineering to destroy. If a pattern of pre-emptive evidence destruction is documented in a current or future crisis investigation, it suggests that the engineered nature of events leaves documentary traces that must be eliminated.
- **Distinguishes from A/B/D:** Model A predicts no systematic evidence destruction (nothing to hide at crisis-initiation level). Model B predicts evidence destruction around aftermath-shaping but not crisis-initiation. Model D predicts no evidence destruction because pipeline behavior is instinctive, not documented.
- **Test:** Track any major crisis investigation for evidence of record destruction, classification abuse, or witness intimidation at the initiation-level (not aftermath-level).

### Falsifying Predictions

**C-FALSIFY-1: If the next major crisis demonstrably damages the most powerful positioned actors — destroying wealth, ending careers, or producing genuine institutional failure among the supposed engineers — Model C is falsified.**
Model C requires that engineers control outcomes. Genuine damage to the most powerful actors (analogous to Bear Stearns destruction in 2008, WC-096) is inconsistent with engineering. The damage must be to CORE actors, not peripheral players or sacrificial nodes.
- **Test:** Track whether the most structurally positioned actors (Big Three, BIS-affiliated central banks, major investment banks) suffer genuine, lasting damage in the next crisis.

**C-FALSIFY-2: If multiple failed elite coordination attempts are documented in real time during the next crisis (analogous to WEF collapse, Great Reset failure, CBDC bifurcation from P6), Model C is falsified.**
Model C requires effective coordination capacity. Real-time documentation of failed coordination — contradictory elite responses, public disagreements among supposed coordinators, institutional collapse of coordination platforms — is inconsistent with the engineering thesis.
- **Test:** Monitor elite coordination platforms (G7, G20, BIS, IMF, successor to WEF) during next crisis for genuine disagreement and failed coordination.

## Model D: Selection (Pipeline Production)

### Core Thesis
Institutional pipelines (S&B/CIA, KA, Siloviki, Princelings, Seminary per Phase 3) produce leaders with specific trait clusters (loyalty, compartmentalization, moral flexibility, crisis exploitation capacity). These leaders do not consciously cultivate crises — they instinctively respond to crises in ways that amplify extraction because the pipeline selected for exactly those traits. The 77% amplification rate reflects trained instinct, not conscious strategy. The system self-organizes because selection produces uniform behavior.

### Confirming Predictions (testable by 2030)

**D-CONFIRM-1: Pipeline graduates will show statistically higher amplification rates than non-pipeline leaders in the next crisis — confirming the 87.5% vs. 50% pattern from P3 (WC-043) with larger sample size.**
Phase 3 found a suggestive but not statistically significant difference (p~0.08) between pipeline and non-pipeline amplification rates. Model D predicts this pattern will replicate with the next crisis providing a larger sample. Specifically: leaders who passed through documented credentialing pipelines (elite university secret societies, intelligence service academies, revolving-door consulting firms, hereditary political dynasties) will choose amplification over stabilization at 80%+ compared to 40-60% for non-pipeline leaders.
- **Distinguishes from A/B/C:** Model A predicts no difference (exploitation is structural, not personnel-dependent). Model B predicts cultivators of any background amplify (not pipeline-specific). Model C predicts engineers control all actors regardless of pipeline (pipeline is irrelevant to engineering thesis).
- **Test:** Map next crisis decision-makers to pipeline affiliation. Score amplification vs. stabilization decisions. Test for statistical significance (Fisher's exact or chi-squared).
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E2 (biographical records, decision records, statistical test)

**D-CONFIRM-2: New pipelines will emerge in AI/tech sector that replicate the five-element architecture documented in P3 (selection gate, bonding mechanism, economic deployment, loyalty enforcement, crisis-compatible output).**
Model D predicts that pipelines are a structural feature of elite systems, not a historical accident. As AI/tech becomes the dominant power domain, new credentialing pipelines should emerge with the same architecture: (1) a selective gate (e.g., elite AI lab admission, YC funding, FAANG early employee cohorts); (2) a bonding mechanism (e.g., shared equity, NDA culture, "move fast and break things" ethos); (3) economic deployment (e.g., board seats, VC partnerships, government advisory roles); (4) loyalty enforcement (e.g., non-compete agreements, IP assignment, social ostracism for critics); (5) crisis-compatible output (e.g., "disruption" as ideology, comfort with creative destruction).
- **Distinguishes from A/B/C:** Model A predicts tech leaders are opportunistic without systematic pipeline production. Model B predicts cultivators recruit from existing pipelines rather than building new ones. Model C predicts tech is controlled by existing power structures, not producing new ones.
- **Test:** Map the credentialing pathway of AI/tech leaders who shape post-crisis architecture. Identify whether a five-element pipeline architecture has crystallized.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E2/E3 (institutional analysis, career mapping)

**D-CONFIRM-3: Pipeline defection rate will remain in the 5-15% range documented in P6 (WC-102), with defectors either marginalized (Sachs pattern), assassinated (Litvinenko pattern), or absorbed back into compatible positions (Scowcroft pattern).**
Model D predicts that pipeline loyalty enforcement is structurally effective but not absolute. The defection rate is a feature, not a bug — it provides the appearance of internal dissent without threatening the pipeline's output. Defectors who survive will be absorbed into positions where their dissent serves pipeline interests (e.g., Sachs becoming a "responsible capitalism" advocate who legitimizes the system he critiques).
- **Distinguishes from A/B/C:** Model A predicts no systematic pattern in defection or its consequences. Model B predicts defection is possible but cultivators respond ad hoc. Model C predicts near-zero defection (engineers have absolute loyalty control).
- **Test:** Track known pipeline graduates who publicly dissent from pipeline consensus during the next crisis. Map their subsequent career trajectories.
- **Evidence Tier if confirmed:** E1/E2 (biographical records, career outcomes)

### Falsifying Predictions

**D-FALSIFY-1: If non-pipeline leaders show EQUAL or HIGHER amplification rates than pipeline graduates in the next crisis, the pipeline-as-selection-mechanism thesis is falsified.**
Model D requires that pipelines produce measurably different behavior. If leaders without pipeline affiliation amplify at the same rate, then amplification is structural (supporting Model A) or cultivated by non-pipeline mechanisms (supporting Model B), not selected for by pipelines.
- **Test:** Same methodology as D-CONFIRM-1, but the falsifying result is p>0.30 (no statistical difference).

**D-FALSIFY-2: If a major pipeline collapses AND its graduates subsequently show different behavioral patterns (reduced amplification, increased stabilization), Model D is falsified.**
Model D predicts that pipeline training produces permanent behavioral orientation — "once a chekist, always a chekist" (RUS-026). If a pipeline's institutional collapse (e.g., CCP loss of power, FSB restructuring, S&B dissolution) produces measurably different behavior in its graduates, then the pipeline was not producing internalized traits but enforcing compliance through institutional pressure. The behavioral change would indicate that pipeline behavior is coerced (Model B: cultivation through institutional pressure) rather than selected (Model D: internalized traits).
- **Test:** Monitor Russian siloviki behavior if/when Putin's personal arbiter role ends. Track whether second-generation siloviki behave differently without the first-generation bonding experience.

## Evidence Gaps and Research Targets

### Gap 1: Pre-Crisis Financial Positioning Data (Distinguishes B from C)
**Current state:** PKG-911 investigated put options but found exploitation without conclusive foreknowledge at E1. PKG-WILDCRAFT Phase 1 found no E1 crisis engineering evidence for any crisis examined.
**What would close it:** Systematic analysis of derivatives positions (options, CDS, currency futures) in the 30-90 day window before each major crisis since 2000, cross-referenced with post-crisis beneficiary mapping. If pre-crisis positioning consistently correlates with post-crisis benefit, Model C gains support. If positioning is absent or random, Models A/B/D are supported.
**Research target:** Financial market microstructure data from CFTC, SEC 13F filings, BIS derivatives statistics. Academic studies of pre-crisis trading patterns (e.g., Poteshman 2006 on 9/11 options).
**Priority:** HIGH — this is the single most important gap between Model B and Model C.

### Gap 2: Pipeline Graduate Career Outcome Database (Distinguishes A from D)
**Current state:** Phase 3 identified 5 pipelines and scored 22 junctures for pipeline affiliation, producing a suggestive but non-significant difference (87.5% vs 50%, p~0.08, WC-043).
**What would close it:** A comprehensive database mapping crisis decision-makers (n>100) to pipeline affiliation, with amplification/stabilization scoring for each decision. This would provide statistical power to confirm or falsify the pipeline effect.
**Research target:** Biographical databases (Marquis Who's Who, Bloomberg Terminal executive bios, SIPRI arms industry personnel), cross-referenced with Yale S&B archives, KA alumni lists, FSB academy records (where available), CCP Central Committee biographical data.
**Priority:** HIGH — this is the single most important gap between Model A and Model D.

### Gap 3: Reform Implementation Process Documentation (Distinguishes A from B)
**Current state:** Phase 4 classified 18 reform episodes by circumvention timing (B/S/A) but relied on post hoc analysis. Real-time documentation of a reform implementation process — including lobbying meetings, rulemaking comments, personnel appointments — would test whether circumvention is genuinely simultaneous or appears simultaneous only in retrospect.
**What would close it:** FOIA requests for lobbying records, rulemaking comment submissions, and meeting logs during the implementation of any major 2026-2030 reform. Track whether the same actors who caused the crisis participate in the reform design AND the subsequent circumvention.
**Research target:** EU AI Act implementation process (2025-2027); Basel III endgame implementation; any post-crisis legislation. OpenSecrets lobbying data, EU Transparency Register, revolving-door databases.
**Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH — this would either confirm the 39% simultaneous rate in real time or reveal it as a retrospective artifact.

### Gap 4: Anti-Fragile Feature Failure Conditions (Distinguishes A from B)
**Current state:** Phase 5 identified 12 anti-fragile features with 8/12 intentionally designed. Phase 6 identified 3 features that FAILED (SWIFT erosion, WEF collapse, dollar hegemony decline). The question: under what conditions do anti-fragile features break?
**What would close it:** Systematic study of conditions under which anti-fragile features degrade. If features break under sufficient external pressure (supporting Model A: no maintenance), or if they break only when maintainers lose political position or die (supporting Model B: actively maintained), the models are distinguished.
**Research target:** Track the 12 Phase 5 features through 2030. Score each for degradation vs. adaptation. Identify whether degradation correlates with loss of specific maintainers or with general systemic stress.
**Priority:** MEDIUM — requires longitudinal tracking.

### Gap 5: Cross-Pipeline Coordination Evidence (Distinguishes B from D)
**Current state:** Phase 3 NF-5 found "no evidence of coordination BETWEEN pipelines" despite documenting five parallel pipelines with identical structural architectures. The only exception is emerging Chinese princeling integration at Western institutions (CHN-027).
**What would close it:** Documentation of cross-pipeline personnel flows, joint coordination venues, or shared operational methodology. If pipelines coordinate (supporting Model B: conscious cultivation across pipelines), the system is actively managed. If pipelines independently converge without coordination (supporting Model D: selection produces convergent behavior without requiring coordination), the convergence is structural.
**Research target:** Track personnel who have passed through MORE than one pipeline (e.g., S&B members who later joined KA; siloviki children educated at Western pipeline institutions). Map Bilderberg, Trilateral Commission, and WEF Young Global Leaders as potential cross-pipeline coordination venues.
**Priority:** MEDIUM — would resolve the "convergent vs. coordinated" question that Phase 3 left open.

## Key Findings

### KF-1: Model B (Cultivation) Produces the Most Testable and Distinguishable Predictions
Model B generates predictions that are distinguishable from ALL three alternatives at every test point. Models A and C produce predictions that are harder to distinguish from each other at specific junctures (both predict either "no pre-positioning" or "total control" — less nuanced). Model D overlaps with B on several predictions but diverges on the mechanism (conscious cultivation vs. trained instinct). The foraging-wildcrafting hybrid is the most analytically productive framework because it generates the most specific, testable predictions.

### KF-2: The 2026-2030 Period Is Unusually Information-Rich for Testing
Three conditions make the next 4 years an exceptionally productive test environment: (1) the CTA gutting (March 2025) creates a natural experiment — if a replacement reform follows the reform-capture-gutting cycle, Model B is strongly confirmed; (2) SWIFT alternatives (CIPS, mBridge) are growing rapidly enough that the dual-track vs. replacement question will resolve by 2030; (3) AI regulation (EU AI Act, US executive orders) provides multiple real-time reform implementation processes to track for simultaneous vs. reactive circumvention.

### KF-3: The Critical Discriminator Is Crisis Initiation Evidence
The single finding that would most powerfully distinguish models is E1 evidence of deliberate crisis engineering (Gap 1). Phase 6 found zero such evidence across all crises examined (WC-094). If this finding holds through 2030 — no E1 evidence of deliberate crisis creation emerging from any source — Model C is progressively falsified, and the B vs. D question becomes the primary analytical frontier. If E1 engineering evidence emerges for ANY crisis, the entire analytical framework requires fundamental revision.

### KF-4: The Pipeline Effect Is the Sharpest A vs. D Discriminator
The 87.5% vs. 50% pipeline amplification difference (WC-043) is currently suggestive but not significant (p~0.08). A single additional crisis producing 15-20 scoreable decisions would provide sufficient statistical power to confirm or falsify. This is the most efficient research investment available: mapping pipeline affiliation of crisis decision-makers requires only biographical research, not classified sources or FOIA litigation.
