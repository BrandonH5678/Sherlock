[E1, 0.95]

3. **Maria Spiropulu** (symposium attendee, Caltech/CERN) was subsequently selected for DARPA's Defense Science Study Group (2020-2021), establishing a documented defense-advisory connection for at least one attendee. No evidence connects this to Epstein or to the Puthoff/Bigelow network. [E1, 0.85]

**Overall Cross-Reference Strength: WEAK (0.10-0.15)** — The Epstein gravity network and the Sherlock advanced propulsion targets are parallel but non-overlapping systems. The absence of connection is itself analytically significant.

## PART II: THOMAS TOWNSEND BROWN NETWORK OVERLAPS

**[E3 | Confidence: 0.05]** No documented connection exists between any Epstein gravity symposium attendee and the Thomas Townsend Brown electrogravitics research lineage. Brown's program operated in the mid-20th century (1930s-1970s), primarily through Naval Research Laboratory connections, Bahnson Labs, and classified Air Force contracts. These are separated by approximately the same distance as naval architecture and fluid dynamics.

## PART VII: ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT

### 7.1 Why These Networks Do Not Overlap

**[E2 | Confidence: 0.75]** The absence of overlap between the Epstein gravity network and the Sherlock advanced propulsion targets is not merely a data gap — it reflects a structural feature of how gravity research is organized in the United States:

1. **Prestige stratification:** Epstein cultivated Nobel laureates and Ivy League/elite institution physicists. The Puthoff/Bigelow/AAWSAP network drew from second-tier institutions (UNLV, EarthTech) and researchers whose work is considered fringe by mainstream physics. These communities do not socialize.

2. **Funding separation:** Epstein's gravity event was private philanthropy directed at theoretical cosmology. The AAWSAP program was classified DIA defense funding directed at engineering applications (propulsion, materials, energy extraction). The Brown electrogravitics program was classified military (Navy/Air Force). These funding streams do not intersect.

3. **Topic disjunction:** "Gravity" at the Epstein symposium meant the cosmological constant, quantum gravity unification, and dark energy. "Gravity" in the Puthoff/Bigelow context meant propulsive engineering — modifying gravitational effects for spacecraft applications. "Gravity" in the Brown context meant electrostatic propulsion effects. Same word, different physics.

4. **Security boundaries:** The Puthoff/Bigelow network operates under classified security protocols. Epstein's conference was unclassified. Brown's work was classified. These security regimes prevent personnel flow.

### 7.2 The One-Way Intellectual Flow

**[E2 | Confidence: 0.80]** The single most significant finding is the one-way intellectual flow: the AAWSAP/DIRD program drew heavily on mainstream theoretical physics (Randall-Sundrum brane models, Thorne wormhole theory, Alcubierre warp metric) as source material for its defense research papers. But this was citation, not collaboration. The mainstream physicists whose work was cited (including Epstein symposium attendees Randall and Thorne) were not consulted, contracted, or aware of their work's application in the classified program. Sean Carroll's characterization of the DIRDs as mainstream physics "dressed up" for defense applications confirms this assessment.

### 7.3 Open Intelligence Questions

1. **Were any Epstein symposium attendees subsequently approached by AAWSAP/BAASS for consultation?** The 2006 conference preceded the 2008 AAWSAP contract by two years. No evidence of such approaches has surfaced, but the question remains open.

2. **Did Epstein maintain post-conference relationships with any attendees who later engaged with defense physics?** Specifically, did Spiropulu's 2020 DARPA DSSG selection have any connection to Epstein network contacts? No evidence suggests this, but Epstein's Feb 2026 file releases are ongoing.

3. **The Isabel Maxwell / Bigelow indirect connection** reported in some sources but not documented to E2 standards — if confirmed, this would represent the closest personnel bridge between Epstein-adjacent figures and the Bigelow/AAWSAP network.

4. **Unidentified symposium attendees:** The 11 confirmed attendees may not represent the complete participant list. The conference brought together "about 20 physicists" according to some accounts. The identities of additional participants are unknown.
