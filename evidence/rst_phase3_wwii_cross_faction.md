## Executive Summary

This report documents the extensive network of American corporate and financial relationships that operated across the Allied-Axis divide before, during, and after World War II. The evidentiary base for these connections is unusually strong — many key claims rest on E1 primary sources including U.S. government seizure orders, congressional investigation records (Truman Committee), declassified intelligence files, and corporate documents subpoenaed by the Department of Justice. The pattern that emerges is not one of isolated opportunism but of systemic cross-faction financial infrastructure that pre-dated the war, survived it, and in many cases was deliberately preserved by the same individuals who then designed the postwar order.

Ten major nodes are documented: (1) Bush/BBH/Thyssen banking, (2) Standard Oil/IG Farben cartel, (3) Sullivan & Cromwell legal facilitation, (4) Ford Motor Company, (5) ITT Corporation, (6) IBM/Dehomag, (7) Chase National Bank, (8) Bank for International Settlements, (9) General Motors/Opel, and (10) the postwar preservation apparatus (Operation Paperclip, Gehlen Organization, McCloy clemency). The connective tissue between these nodes is dense — Sullivan & Cromwell represented multiple corporate actors, the Dulles brothers appear at critical junctions throughout, and the postwar order was designed in significant part by individuals with direct cross-faction financial interests.

Key structural finding: The Bretton Woods resolution to liquidate the BIS (passed 1944, reversed 1948) and McCloy's mass clemency at Landsberg (1951) represent the two decisive moments where postwar accountability was subordinated to Cold War strategic imperatives. Both decisions involved individuals with pre-war cross-faction ties.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Bush/BBH/Thyssen — Union Banking Corporation Seizure
**Evidence Tier:** E1
**Confidence:** 0.95

On October 20, 1942, the U.S. government seized Union Banking Corporation (UBC) under the Trading with the Enemy Act via Vesting Order No. 248, signed by Alien Property Custodian Leo T. Crowley. UBC had been established as a joint venture between the Harriman banking interests and Fritz Thyssen, the German industrialist who financed Hitler's rise.

Prescott Bush was one of seven directors of UBC. All seven directors' shares were held as nominees for Bank voor Handel en Scheepvaart N.V. in Rotterdam, which was owned by the Thyssen family. W. Averell Harriman and E. Roland Harriman were partners in the web of Thyssen-controlled ventures. Brown Brothers Harriman (formed 1931 from the merger of Brown Brothers & Co. and W.A. Harriman & Co.) served as the institutional nexus.

Within eight days of the UBC seizure, two additional Bush-Harriman-Thyssen entities were seized: Holland-American Trading Corporation (Vesting Order No. 261) and Seamless Steel Equipment Corporation (Vesting Order No. 259). In November 1942, the Silesian-American Corporation was also seized.

Critical scope limitation: A July 1942 government investigation found that allegations of UBC holding gold on behalf of Nazi leaders were NOT substantiated. The seizure was based on Thyssen ownership/control, not on direct Nazi gold holding. Bush's role was as a director and nominee shareholder — the evidence establishes him as a functionary of the Harriman-Thyssen financial relationship, not as an independent Nazi sympathizer.

Newly discovered U.S. government documents (reported by The Guardian, 2004) reveal Bush failed to divest from more than a dozen "enemy national" relationships that continued until as late as 1951.

### Finding 2: Standard Oil / IG Farben Cartel Agreements
**Evidence Tier:** E1
**Confidence:** 0.95

The Standard Oil of New Jersey – IG Farben cartel relationship is one of the best-documented cross-faction arrangements of the era, based on 40,000 corporate documents reviewed by the Department of Justice and presented to the Truman Committee (Senate Special Committee to Investigate the National Defense Program).

Key documented elements:
- **November 1929**: Standard Oil and IG Farben signed a cartel agreement dividing the world — IG Farben received control of chemicals, Standard Oil received petroleum products and synthetic gasoline.
- **1930**: Joint American Study Company (JASCO) formed as patent-sharing vehicle. Through JASCO, German Buna synthetic rubber technology came to America — but IG Farben retained the right to refuse licensing of buna rubber patents in the United States.
- **October 1939**: After war began in Europe, Standard and IG Farben reached a further understanding for means of working together during the war "whether or not the U.S. came in."
- **Patent transfer**: Standard turned all its chemical patents over to IG Farben, including the invaluable butyl rubber process.
- **Rubber suppression**: IG Farben refused to allow licensing of buna rubber patents to American manufacturers, depriving the U.S. of synthetic rubber capability.

Assistant Attorney General Thurman Arnold presented the full documentary record to the Truman Committee in late March 1942. Senator Truman called the arrangement "Treason." The Committee concluded that Standard Oil "did hamper the development of synthetic rubber in the United States." On March 25, 1942, Standard Oil signed a consent decree, released 2,000 patents royalty-free, and paid a $50,000 fine — a penalty widely regarded as trivial relative to the strategic damage.

### Finding 3: Sullivan & Cromwell as Cross-Faction Legal Infrastructure
**Evidence Tier:** E1-E2
**Confidence:** 0.90

Sullivan & Cromwell, where both John Foster Dulles and Allen Dulles practiced, served as the primary American legal facilitator of pre-war German industrial finance. This finding supplements the existing PKG-MK Dulles portfolio (ID 7, 0.85-0.90 confidence) by documenting the pre-CIA corporate infrastructure.

Documented clients and activities:
- Floated the first American bonds issued by Krupp A.G.
- Represented IG Farben's American subsidiary, General Aniline & Film
- Extended IG Farben's global reach through legal structuring
- Successfully fought to block Canada's effort to restrict steel exports to German arms makers
- John Foster Dulles brought IG Farben into an international nickel cartel (1934), giving Nazi Germany access to nickel critical for warfare
- John Foster Dulles became the principal U.S. broker for German municipal and corporate bonds, totaling over $1 billion in the late 1920s

After the Nazi Party came to power (1933), John Foster Dulles required Berlin staff to sign "Heil Hitler!" on all outgoing Sullivan & Cromwell correspondence. Junior partners forced Dulles to cut German business ties in 1935 — but this was driven by optics, not principle.

In April 1933, Allen Dulles met Hitler in Berlin on State Department duty and wrote to his brother that conditions under Hitler's regime were "not quite as bad" as reported.

As the Third Reich neared defeat (1944-1945), Dulles and Sullivan & Cromwell worked with German industrialists to move Nazi funds out of German territory. Dulles and Kurt von Schröder created companies to transfer Nazi wealth to other nations. This directly connects to Operation Sunrise (Finding 9).

### Finding 4: Ford Motor Company — Dual-Faction Production
**Evidence Tier:** E1-E2
**Confidence:** 0.90

Ford Motor Company maintained simultaneous production for both sides of the conflict through its German subsidiary Ford-Werke (renamed from Ford Motor Company A.G. in 1939).

**Personal ideology**: Henry Ford was the only American mentioned favorably in Mein Kampf. Ford's newspaper, The Dearborn Independent, published 91 issues of antisemitic content (1920-1927), compiled into "The International Jew" — 500,000 copies distributed. In 1938, Ford accepted the Grand Cross of the Supreme Order of the German Eagle from Nazi officials, the highest award bestowed on foreigners.

**German operations (Ford-Werke)**:
- By 1941, Ford-Werke ceased passenger vehicle production and devoted entire capacity to military trucks for the Wehrmacht
- Ford-Werke and GM's Opel together provided most of the trucks that motorized the Nazi war machine
- Ford-Werke manufactured turbines for V-2 rockets
- Ford-Werke employed slave laborers from Buchenwald concentration camp — this was NOT required by the Nazi regime; it was a voluntary corporate decision under Robert Hans Schmidt
- Ford-Werke engaged in illegal manufacture of munitions

**American operations**: Ford built the Willow Run plant near Michigan — the largest assembly line in the world at the time — producing B-24 Liberator bombers for the U.S. military. By early 1944, Willow Run was the leading producer of heavy bombers.

The simultaneous operation of Ford-Werke (building trucks and V-2 components for Germany) and Willow Run (building bombers for the Allies) represents one of the most concrete examples of cross-faction industrial production.

### Finding 5: ITT Corporation — Documented Double-Agent Corporate Structure
**Evidence Tier:** E2
**Confidence:** 0.80

ITT Corporation under CEO Sosthenes Behn operated the most explicitly documented dual-loyalty corporate structure of the war.

- **August 3, 1933**: Behn met Hitler in Berlin — one of the first meetings between Hitler and a U.S. businessman
- ITT acquired a minority share in Focke-Wulf Flugzeugbau through German financier Kurt Baron von Schröder. By 1943, ITT became the largest shareholder (29%) of Focke-Wulf, the manufacturer of the Fw 190 fighter
- ITT subsidiaries made cash payments to SS leader Heinrich Himmler through the "Circle of Friends" (Freundeskreis)
- ITT-Focke-Wulf planes bombed Allied ships while simultaneously ITT direction-finders were saving other ships from torpedoes

Behn operated as a de facto double agent: reporting on German company activities to the U.S. government while simultaneously contributing money to Himmler's SS and recruiting Nazis onto ITT's board. This cross-faction position was documented by Anthony Sampson in "The Sovereign State of ITT" (1973).

The ITT/Schröder connection is significant: Kurt von Schröder was the same banker through whom Allen Dulles moved Nazi funds (Finding 3).

### Finding 6: IBM / Dehomag — Technology as Enabler of Genocide
**Evidence Tier:** E1-E2
**Confidence:** 0.90

Edwin Black's "IBM and the Holocaust" (2001, expanded editions) documents IBM's strategic technology alliance with the Third Reich based on extensive archival research. Won the American Society of Journalists and Authors award for Best Non-Fiction Book of the Year (2003).

Key documented facts:
- IBM owned and controlled Dehomag (Deutsche Hollerith Maschinen Gesellschaft) outright from 1923
- IBM leased (never sold) its tabulation machines, retaining control over punch card supply, spare parts, and on-site servicing — including at concentration camps
- Each punch card series was custom-designed by IBM engineers to capture specific Nazi data requirements
- The 1933 census, using IBM machines, raised the official estimate of Jews in Germany from ~500,000 to ~2 million
- The 1937 census was "the final and decisive identification step" for tracing Jewish ancestry under Nuremberg race laws
- Card sorting operations were established in every major concentration camp
- Applications extended to military logistics, ghetto statistics, train traffic management, and concentration camp capacity

**Thomas Watson Sr.** received the Merit Cross of the German Eagle with Star from the Nazi regime in June 1937 — second in prestige only to the Grand Cross. Watson wanted to distance IBM from Germany after the 1938 pogroms but was convinced not to by Secretary of State Cordell Hull; Watson did not withdraw until spring 1940.

The lease-not-sell model meant IBM maintained ongoing operational relationships with the machinery being used in the Holocaust — this was not a one-time sale but a continuous service provision.

### Finding 7: Chase National Bank — Rückwanderer Mark Scheme and Occupied Paris
**Evidence Tier:** E1-E2
**Confidence:** 0.85

Chase National Bank (predecessor to JPMorgan Chase) maintained documented financial operations with the Nazi regime before and during WWII.

**Rückwanderer Mark Scheme (1936-1941)**:
- Chase assisted the Nazi government in selling special German marks to U.S. residents of German descent
- Between 1936 and 1941, the scheme generated over $20 million for the Nazi government
- Chase National Bank and its sub-agents earned over $500,000 in commissions
- The scheme continued until Roosevelt's Executive Order freezing German assets on June 14, 1941
- After the freeze order, Chase reportedly unblocked accounts within hours and funds were transferred through South America to Nazi Germany

**Paris operations**: Chase kept its Paris branch open throughout the Nazi occupation of France. The branch conducted business with and for Nazi occupiers, including seizing the accounts of Jewish customers and funding Gestapo activities against the French population.

Notably, National City Bank (predecessor to Citibank) shuttered its European offices — providing a direct comparison for corporate decision-making under similar conditions.

This evidence was documented in Cambridge University Press publication "U.S. Intelligence and the Nazis" (chapter: "Banking on Hitler: Chase National Bank and the Rückwanderer Mark Scheme, 1936-1941") and was the subject of a 1998 lawsuit.

### Finding 8: Bank for International Settlements — Cross-Faction Central Banking
**Evidence Tier:** E1
**Confidence:** 0.90

The BIS, headquartered in Basel, Switzerland, operated as the most structurally significant cross-faction financial institution during WWII, with documented involvement from both Allied and Axis central banks.

**Nazi gold transfers**: The BIS received 3.7 tonnes of gold from the Reichsbank that had been looted from the central banks of Belgium and the Netherlands. The gold had been remelted at the Prussian mint to conceal its origin. The BIS cooperated with the postwar Tripartite Commission investigation and returned the 3.7 tonnes in 1948.

**Thomas McKittrick (BIS President 1940-1946)**:
- American banker who served as BIS president throughout the war
- OSS codename 644 — regularly met with Allen Dulles (also based in Switzerland) and American Ambassador Leland Harrison
- Under McKittrick, the BIS accepted looted Nazi gold, carried out foreign exchange deals for the Reichsbank, and recognized Nazi invasions/annexations of conquered countries
- Simultaneously provided intelligence to Dulles and Washington — another documented double-agent position

**Bretton Woods Resolution (1944)**: The conference voted to liquidate the BIS "at the earliest possible moment." The Norwegian delegation presented evidence the BIS was guilty of war crimes. Roosevelt, Morgenthau, and Harry Dexter White all supported dissolution. John Maynard Keynes opposed. The resolution was never implemented — Truman ended U.S. involvement in the dissolution effort in April 1945, and the decision was officially reversed in 1948.

**Postwar career trajectory**: McKittrick left the BIS in 1946 and was appointed vice president of Chase National Bank (Finding 7), then senior VP and director. Averell Harriman (Finding 1) invited McKittrick to administer what became the Marshall Plan. The advisory committee for this included Owen Young (architect of pre-war German debt arrangements) and Allen Dulles.

This represents perhaps the single most important personnel continuity: BIS president during wartime Nazi cooperation → Chase Bank VP → Marshall Plan administration, with Allen Dulles and Averell Harriman as the facilitators at every stage.

### Finding 9: General Motors / Opel — Parallel Military Production
**Evidence Tier:** E1-E2
**Confidence:** 0.85

General Motors' wholly-owned subsidiary Adam Opel AG became an indispensable supplier to the German armed forces, producing over 130,000 Opel Blitz trucks and chassis through 1944 at the purpose-built Opelwerk Brandenburg facility (constructed 1935).

- GM was the owner of Germany's largest automobile factory
- GM's German plants built thousands of bomber and jet fighter propulsion systems for the Luftwaffe while its American plants produced aircraft engines for the U.S. Army Air Corps
- Communications and material reportedly continued to flow between GM plants in Allied and Axis territories for the duration of the war
- GM maintained management control of the Russelsheim warplane factory for nearly a full year after Germany's declaration of war against the U.S. (December 11, 1941)
- The Reich appointed an administrator for the plant in November 1942 — ten months after the U.S. and Germany were at war

As documented by researchers: "Due to their concentrated economic power over motor vehicle production in both Allied and Axis territories, the Big Three inevitably became major factors in the preparations and progress of the war, with multinational dominance making GM and Ford principal suppliers for the forces of fascism as well as for the forces of democracy."

### Finding 10: Operation Paperclip — Institutional Preservation of Nazi Scientific Assets
**Evidence Tier:** E1
**Confidence:** 0.95

Operation Paperclip (1945-1959) transferred more than 1,600 German scientists, engineers, and technicians to the United States. Approximately 1,500 professionals and their families were relocated. JIOA records consist of personnel dossiers on over 1,500 individuals. President Truman officially approved the expanded program on September 3, 1946.

**Documented whitewashing**: JIOA and OSS officials bypassed Truman's directive against recruiting Nazi Party members by eliminating or falsifying incriminating evidence from scientists' records. This is documented in National Archives files.

**Key personnel and their Nazi affiliations**:
- **Wernher von Braun**: Nazi Party and SS member/officer. Became first director of NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center. Designed Saturn V rocket for Apollo program.
- **Kurt Debus**: Former SS member. Became first director of Kennedy Space Center. NASA removed his name from facilities after public scrutiny.
- **Arthur Rudolph**: Chief engineer of Saturn V. Surrendered U.S. citizenship to avoid prosecution by Office of Special Investigations (OSI).
- **Hubertus Strughold**: Chief of aeromedical research for Göring's Luftwaffe. Called "Father of Space Medicine." Designed pressure suits and life support for Gemini and Apollo. Posthumously linked to human experimentation at Dachau — never charged.
- **Walter Schreiber**: Chief Nazi medical officer involved in human experiments at concentration camps (typhus injection, sterilization, freezing, low-pressure chambers). Arrived in U.S. 1951; after Boston Globe exposure, emigrated to Argentina with U.S. military assistance.
- **Erich Traub**: Nazi biological weapons researcher from Riems Island facility. Under Paperclip contract, consulted with Fort Detrick (Army biological warfare HQ). Visited Plum Island Animal Disease Center at least three times in the 1950s. Provided groundwork for Fort Detrick's offshore germ warfare animal disease lab.

U.S. Air Force sponsored the largest number of Paperclip scientists: 260 men, of whom 36 returned to Germany.

### Finding 11: The Gehlen Organization — Nazi Intelligence Infrastructure Preserved
**Evidence Tier:** E1
**Confidence:** 0.90

Reinhard Gehlen, head of Nazi military intelligence for the Eastern Front (Fremde Heere Ost), surrendered to U.S. forces in May 1945 with his intelligence files on the Soviet Union. He brokered a deal: instead of war crimes prosecution, he and his team would establish a secret intelligence service for the U.S. occupation forces.

- The Gehlen Organization employed "numerous former Nazis and known war criminals" (American National Security Archive assessment)
- Recruited thousands of former Gestapo, Wehrmacht, and SS officers including: Alois Brunner (Eichmann's most trusted aide), SS Major Emil Augsburg (oversaw murder of hundreds of Russians), Klaus Barbie ("Butcher of Lyon")
- Provided an estimated 70% of U.S. intelligence on the USSR and Eastern Europe
- Transformed into the Bundesnachrichtendienst (BND), West Germany's Federal Intelligence Service, in 1956
- Gehlen served as first BND president until retirement in 1968
- Many ex-Nazi functionaries, including the captor of Anne Frank, transferred from the Gehlen Organization to the BND

CIA declassified records on Gehlen (released 2000-2001 under the Nazi War Crimes Disclosure Act) confirmed these relationships.

### Finding 12: McCloy Clemency — Institutional Rehabilitation of Nazi War Criminals
**Evidence Tier:** E1
**Confidence:** 0.95

John J. McCloy, Wall Street lawyer (Cadwalader then Cravath), Assistant Secretary of War during WWII, served as first U.S. High Commissioner for Germany (September 1949 - August 1952).

On January 31, 1951, McCloy commuted death sentences for 21 Nazi war criminals and reduced terms for dozens more at Landsberg Prison:
- **Alfried Krupp**: Released along with 8 board members. Property valued at ~45 million and companies fully restored.
- **All IG Farben executives**: Free by February 1951, including Fritz Ter Meer, senior executive of the company that produced Zyklon B.
- **Hermann Schmitz** (IG Farben chairman): Released from prison in 1950.

McCloy maintained five death sentences for those he called "the worst of the worst" (Pohl, Ohlendorf, Blobel, Braune, Naumann) and rejected blanket amnesty requests.

The Cold War context is documented: the Korean War (June 1950) shifted strategic calculations. Germany was to be rebuilt as an industrial bulwark against the Soviet bloc. McCloy and Allen Dulles "worked to free numerous Nazi industrialists and bankers and return them to positions of great economic power and influence — in particular the men who ran I.G. Farben" (The Critic Magazine, citing primary sources).

The McKittrick-to-Chase-to-Marshall Plan pipeline (Finding 8) and the McCloy clemency (this finding) represent the two primary mechanisms by which cross-faction personnel were rehabilitated into the postwar Western institutional order.

The architects of the postwar order had extensive pre-war and wartime cross-faction connections. This is not a conspiracy theory — it is a documented personnel continuity.

Key figures and their cross-faction positions:

- **John J. McCloy**: Wall Street lawyer → Asst. Secretary of War → High Commissioner Germany → Chairman of Chase Manhattan Bank (1953-1960) → Chairman of the Council on Foreign Relations → Warren Commission member.
- **Averell Harriman**: Brown Brothers Harriman (Thyssen partnership) → Ambassador to USSR → Ambassador to UK → Secretary of Commerce → Governor of New York. Facilitated McKittrick's Marshall Plan role.
- **Thomas McKittrick**: BIS President (wartime) → Chase National Bank VP → Marshall Plan administration. Dulles and Harriman as sponsors at each transition.

The Bretton Woods decision to liquidate the BIS (1944) was reversed by 1948. The decision to denazify German industry was reversed by 1951. In both cases, the individuals reversing the decisions had pre-existing financial relationships with the entities being preserved.

## Negative Findings

### NF-1: UBC Gold Holding Allegation — NOT SUBSTANTIATED
The July 1942 investigation into whether Union Banking Corporation held gold on behalf of Nazi leaders found NO evidence to support this claim. The seizure was based on Thyssen family ownership/control through Bank voor Handel en Scheepvaart, not on direct Nazi gold custody. Some secondary sources conflate the UBC seizure with gold-related allegations — this is incorrect per the government's own investigation record.

### NF-2: Prescott Bush as Independent Nazi Sympathizer — NOT SUPPORTED BY EVIDENCE
Bush's role at UBC was as a director and nominee shareholder for the Harriman-Thyssen financial relationship. The evidence establishes him as a functionary, not as an ideological Nazi sympathizer. Claims that Bush "funded Hitler's rise to power" overstate his role — the Thyssen-Harriman relationship predated Bush's involvement, and Bush held nominee shares, not beneficial ownership. The failure to divest until 1951 (RST-053) is more accurately characterized as negligence or financial self-interest than as ideological alignment.

### NF-3: Standard Oil Executives Convicted of Treason — DID NOT OCCUR
Despite Senator Truman calling the Standard Oil-IG Farben arrangement "Treason," no Standard Oil executive was ever convicted of treason or any criminal offense beyond the consent decree. The $50,000 fine was the only penalty. This gap between rhetorical condemnation and legal consequence is itself analytically significant.

### NF-4: IBM Direct Knowledge of Holocaust Application — DISPUTED
While Edwin Black's research documents IBM's technology provision and the lease-not-sell model that maintained operational relationships, IBM has contested the claim that Thomas Watson Sr. or IBM headquarters had direct knowledge of how Dehomag machines were being used in the Holocaust specifically (as opposed to general census and logistics). The evidence is strong for corporate facilitation and operational continuity; the question of specific headquarters knowledge of genocide application remains disputed.

### NF-5: Ford-Werke Under Direct Ford HQ Control During War — CONTESTED
The degree to which Ford Motor Company headquarters in Dearborn maintained operational control over Ford-Werke after the U.S. entered the war is contested. Ford's own commissioned report acknowledged the slave labor and military production but disputed the degree of HQ direction. The voluntary use of slave labor is documented, but the chain of authorization to Dearborn is not conclusively established.

### NF-6: Coordinated "Grand Conspiracy" — NOT THE CORRECT ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK
The evidence does not support a unified conspiracy theory in which all cross-faction actors coordinated through a single command structure. The pattern is better described as convergent institutional self-interest: multiple corporations, banks, and law firms independently maintained profitable relationships across the conflict line, facilitated by a small network of common intermediaries (Sullivan & Cromwell, Schröder, the BIS). The postwar rehabilitation was semi-coordinated through the Dulles-McCloy-Harriman network, but the wartime cross-faction operations were largely independent corporate decisions made within shared structural incentives.

## Analytical Assessment

### Structural Pattern: The "Revolving Door of Rehabilitation"

The most significant finding is the personnel continuity between wartime cross-faction operations and postwar institutional design:

1. **Sullivan & Cromwell** (1920s-1930s): Legal facilitation of German industrial finance
2. **Wartime** (1939-1945): Cross-faction operations continue; some actors serve as intelligence sources
3. **Postwar** (1945-1952): Same individuals design denazification policy, then reverse it
4. **Cold War institutions**: Rehabilitated personnel placed in new institutional positions

The critical pivot point is 1950-1951: the Korean War created the strategic rationale for reversing denazification. But the personnel making these decisions (Dulles, McCloy, Harriman) had pre-existing financial relationships with the entities being preserved. This is not speculation — it is documented biographical fact.

### Evidence Quality Assessment

This topic has unusually strong primary source evidence:
- **E1 sources**: Vesting Orders (National Archives), Truman Committee records, DOJ consent decrees, Bretton Woods Conference records, JIOA dossiers, OSS records, High Commissioner records
- **E2 sources**: Edwin Black's archival research, Kinzer biography, Cambridge University Press publications, Sampson investigation
- **E3 sources**: Some claims about specific knowledge (IBM HQ awareness, Ford HQ control) remain at E3

The overall evidentiary density is higher than any other Sherlock campaign phase to date.
