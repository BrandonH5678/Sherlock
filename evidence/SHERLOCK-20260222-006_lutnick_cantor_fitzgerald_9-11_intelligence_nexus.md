## 3. 9/11 FINANCIAL FOREKNOWLEDGE INVESTIGATION

### 3.1 Documented Put Options Activity

**Claim:** Unusually high volumes of put options were purchased on American Airlines and United Airlines stock in the days before September 11, 2001.

**Documented Facts:**
- Surges in put options (instruments that profit when stock drops) on:
  - **United Airlines (UAL):** September 6-7, 2001
  - **American Airlines (AMR):** September 10, 2001
- The International Organisation of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) stated the pre-9/11 financial maneuvers amounted to "several hundred million dollars" and constituted **"the most important crime of insider trading ever committed"**

**Official Explanation (9/11 Commission & SEC):**
- "Exhaustive investigations by the Securities and Exchange Commission, FBI, and other agencies have uncovered no evidence that anyone with advance knowledge of the attacks profited through securities transactions"
- SEC examined more than **9.5 million securities transactions** in weeks preceding 9/11
- Reviewed trading in 103 companies across 6 industry groups
- 95% of UAL puts on September 6 were traced to a single U.S.-based institutional investor "with no conceivable ties to al Qaeda" -- described as part of a broader trading strategy that also included buying 115,000 shares of American Airlines on September 10
- American Airlines trading on September 10 traced to a U.S.-based options trading newsletter faxed to subscribers on September 9

**Contradicting Academic Research:**
- **Allen M. Poteshman (2006)**, "Unusual Option Market Activity and the Terrorist Attacks of September 11, 2001," *The Journal of Business*, Vol. 79, No. 4, pp. 1703-1726 (University of Chicago Press, peer-reviewed)
- Conclusion: "there is evidence of unusual option market activity in the days leading up to September 11 that is consistent with investors trading on advance knowledge of the attacks"
- Found an "unusually high level of put buying" that was statistically significant

**Additional Suspicious Trading (Kevin Ryan research):**
- FBI briefing document from 2003 (declassified 2009) described suspicious purchase of **56,000 shares of Stratesec** in days prior to 9/11
- Stratesec provided security systems for Dulles Airport, the World Trade Center, AND United Airlines
- Stratesec share price **nearly doubled** when markets reopened September 17

**[E1 | Confidence: 0.85]** The unusual trading activity itself is documented fact, confirmed by both the SEC and academic research. The dispute is over interpretation: the 9/11 Commission attributed it to innocent actors, while peer-reviewed academic analysis found patterns "consistent with" foreknowledge. The SEC investigation's conclusion rests on identifying specific traders; the Poteshman study analyzes statistical patterns. Both can be simultaneously true -- the identified traders may have been innocent while other unidentified trading was informed. **The identity of the "single institutional investor" responsible for 95% of the UAL puts has never been publicly disclosed.**
