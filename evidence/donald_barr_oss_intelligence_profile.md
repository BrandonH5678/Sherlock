## 4. CIA CONNECTION — WHAT IS AND IS NOT DOCUMENTED

### Documented: NOTHING

There is **no documented evidence** that Donald Barr continued with the CIA after the OSS was dissolved (September 1945) or after the CIA was created (September 1947).

### Frequently Claimed But Unsupported

Multiple sources (Substack articles, social media, investigative blogs) **claim or imply** Donald Barr had CIA connections. These claims rely on:

1. The fact that OSS was the precursor to the CIA (true, but not all OSS personnel transitioned)
2. His son William Barr later worked at the CIA (1971-1977) — but this does not prove the father had CIA ties
3. His institutional positions at Columbia (where CIA historically recruited) — circumstantial
4. His hiring of Epstein — retroactive inference, not evidence of CIA involvement

### The William Barr CIA Connection (Father-Son)

- William Barr began at the CIA as a summer intern in **1971** while an undergraduate at Columbia
- He worked full-time at the CIA from **1973-1977**: first as an analyst in the Intelligence Directorate (China analysis), then in the Office of Legislative Counsel as a congressional liaison
- William Barr was at the CIA during the **Church Committee and Pike Committee** investigations (1975-1976), serving as agency liaison to Congress during these investigations
- He left after Jimmy Carter was elected (1977)
- One source claims CIA employment began as early as June 4, 1971

**Evidence Grade on Donald Barr CIA link:** E3 (speculative inference from circumstantial connections)
**Evidence Grade on William Barr CIA employment:** E1 (documented in DOJ biography, Senate confirmations, Barr's own memoir)

**Evidence Grade:** E1 (Columbia University records, NSF records, biographical databases)

### Dalton School Headmaster (1964-1974)

- Appointed headmaster of the Dalton School, Manhattan's Upper East Side, in **1964**
- Brought a **strict disciplinarian approach** to what was a progressive institution
- Expelled five sixth-grade students in 1970 (four for extortion, one for stealing $15 from a teacher)
- Resisted demands from students, parents, and trustees for reduced discipline amid 1960s/70s permissiveness
- Came into escalating conflict with the board of trustees
- **February 1974:** Announced his resignation, effective end of school year (June 1974)
- Wrote to faculty/parents: "Everyone knows that I am somewhat anachronistic in my views of the educational leadership of a school"
- The dispute "seemed to center on the question of where the board's authority should yield to the headmaster's judgment"
- Vanity Fair investigation: Barr was effectively **fired** — he was "allowed legally to resign"

**Evidence Grade:** E1-E2 (TIME Magazine 1974, Vanity Fair 2019, NPR 2019, Snopes fact-check, multiple news sources)

### Hackley School (1975-1986)

- Became headmaster of **Hackley School, Tarrytown, New York** in September 1975
- Received stronger support from parents and trustees than at Dalton
- Dismissed 28 students in June 1976, expelled 3 more in February 1977 for disruptive behavior/substandard performance

**Evidence Grade:** E1 (Hackley School records, biographical databases)

### The Critical Question: Who Actually Hired Epstein?

**Snopes found:** "It's not clear whether Barr had any role in hiring Epstein." The key ambiguity is that Barr announced his resignation in February 1974 but remained headmaster until June 1974. Epstein started in September 1974. The hiring decision could have been made:

1. **Before June 1974** (while Barr was still headmaster) — POSSIBLE but unproven
2. **During summer 1974** (after Barr left, before school started) — by interim headmaster Peter Branch or the hiring committee

**Key contextual factors:**
- Dalton's math department **lost four teachers** before the 1974-75 school year — creating urgent staffing needs
- Barr was known for hiring "young people in their early-to-mid-20s whom he saw as smart and energetic"
- Private schools like Dalton do not require state teaching licenses or certificates
- Epstein reportedly **fabricated academic qualifications**, claiming degrees from Cooper Union and NYU

**Evidence Grade on "Barr hired Epstein":** E2-E3 (Widely repeated but Snopes rates as "unproven." The temporal overlap makes it plausible but not certain. No Dalton employment records have been publicly released.)

### How Unusual Was a No-Degree Hire?

- **For a public school:** Extremely unusual to impossible (state licensing required)
- **For Dalton specifically:** Unusual but not impossible — private schools are exempt from state licensing requirements, and Barr specifically preferred unconventional hires
- **In context:** The math department losing four teachers created emergency conditions that may have lowered standards

**Evidence Grade:** E2 (multiple former faculty/alumni confirm the practice)

- **Published:** September 17, 1973 by Charterhouse (distributed by McKay)
- **Reprinted:** February 1975 by Fawcett Crest Books

### Plot Summary

Set in an interplanetary empire ruled by aristocrats, the story follows ambassador **John Craig**, who is captured by interplanetary pirates and sold into slavery on the planet **Kossar** — a colony settled by the "Carlyle Society" as a place of exile for political extremists, now ruled by an oligarchical high council of seven nobles.

Craig spends two years as a slave of **Lady Morgan Sidney**, the only female member of the oligarchy — described as "beautiful, sensual, and sadistic." Together they rule over and engage in sexual relations with those under their dominion, **including an enslaved teenager at a clinic used to breed enslaved people**.

The novel has been described as depicting:
- **Rape of enslaved people, particularly teenage girls**
- Other coercive sex acts
- An oligarchic system that enables sex slavery
- A breeding/trafficking operation for enslaved people

### Connections Drawn to Epstein

The parallels noted by researchers and media:
1. **Oligarchs engaging in sex slavery** parallels Epstein's operation serving elites
2. **Enslaved teenagers** parallels Epstein's trafficking of minors
3. **A system where powerful figures are protected** parallels institutional cover-up of Epstein
4. **Published one year before Epstein was hired at Barr's school**

### What the Evidence Does NOT Show

- That Space Relations was anything more than a dark science fiction novel

The circumstantial chain is extensive enough to warrant investigation but insufficient for conclusions. The most important unresolved question remains: **what did Donald Barr actually do in the OSS**, and **did he maintain intelligence connections afterward**? Without his OSS personnel file and any post-war intelligence records, the chain from OSS-to-Dalton-to-Epstein remains speculative.
