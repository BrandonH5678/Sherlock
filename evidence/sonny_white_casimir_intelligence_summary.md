## Key Claims

### CLAIM-SW-001: Casimir Chips Generate Continuous Power from Quantum Vacuum
- **Source:** JRE #2318, casimirspace.com, Tim Ventura/Medium
- **Evidence Tier:** E1 (first-person testimony) / E3 (unverified commercial claims)
- **Content:** Casimir Inc. has developed nanoscale chips containing millions of Casimir cavities etched onto silicon substrate. Modified cavity design uses electrically-isolated conductive pillars on fixed substrates to prevent wall collapse. Chips reportedly produce continuous electrical output.
- **Demonstrated performance (as of May 2025):** ~30 mV steady-state voltage; 3.5V capacitive discharges in shielded enclosures
- **Target performance:** 1.5V, 25 microamps per chip (~37.5 microwatts); chip dimensions 5mm x 5mm
- **White's description:** "A solar panel that works in the dark" / "A battery that never needs to be recharged"
- **CRITICAL: No peer-reviewed publication validates the energy extraction claims. No independent laboratory verification.**

### CLAIM-SW-002: DARPA-Funded Casimir Research Accidentally Produced Warp Bubble Geometry
- **Source:** The Debrief (Dec 2021), EPJC paper (May 2021), AIAA presentation (Aug 2021)
- **Evidence Tier:** E1 (peer-reviewed publication)
- **Content:** While analyzing custom Casimir cavity geometries for DARPA Defense Sciences Office, White's team found that a specific nano/microstructure (1-micron sphere in 4-micron cylinder) produces negative vacuum energy density distribution matching the Alcubierre warp metric requirements.
- **Paper:** "Worldline numerics applied to custom Casimir geometry generates unanticipated intersection with Alcubierre warp metric" — EPJC Vol. 81, Issue 7 (2021)
- **Co-authors:** Jerry Vera, Arum Han, Alexander R. Bruccoleri, Jonathan MacArthur
- **White's statement:** "To be clear, our finding is not a warp bubble analog, it is a real, albeit humble and tiny, warp bubble."
- **Critical reception:** Ethan Siegel rebuttal: mathematical correspondence ≠ physical warp bubble. The "discovery" is a numerical prediction based on White's dynamic vacuum model, not an experimental measurement of spacetime curvature.

### CLAIM-SW-003: Dynamic Vacuum Acoustic Model Derives Hydrogen Spectrum with Zero Free Parameters
- **Source:** Physical Review Research Vol. 8, 013264 (March 9, 2026)
- **Evidence Tier:** E1 (peer-reviewed, APS journal)
- **Content:** Treats the quantum vacuum as a compressible acoustic medium. Adding quadratic temporal dispersion produces hydrogenic eigenfunctions — the exact hydrogen orbitals. Derives complete Rydberg ladder, orbital shapes, isotope shifts, Stark/Zeeman analogues.
- **Authors:** Harold White, Jerry Vera, Andre Sylvester, Leonard Dudinski (all Casimir Inc.)
- **Key claim:** Zero free parameters — quantization emerges from wave resonance in vacuum around a proton
- **Critical reception:** Sabine Hossenfelder: 9/10 probability incorrect, "weird reformulation of standard QM." Hans Jonsson: circular reasoning — imports h-bar and reduced mass, then "derives" the spectrum those constants define. Isospectral mapping, not independent derivation.
- **CRITICAL: Paper contains NO experimental data, NO energy extraction claims, NO mention of chips or power generation.**

### CLAIM-SW-004: Kelvin Probe Confirmation of Pillar Voltage Differential
- **Source:** casimirspace.com, JRE #2318
- **Evidence Tier:** E2 (company-reported experimental result)
- **Content:** Using Kelvin Probe Force Microscopy, the team confirmed that conductive pillars in modified Casimir cavities sit at lower voltage potential relative to cavity walls, as predicted by White's dynamic vacuum model. This voltage differential is the basis for the energy extraction claim.
- **Significance:** If validated independently, this would be the experimental bridge between the theoretical model and the energy extraction technology.

### CLAIM-SW-005: Atomic Orbitals Are Acoustic Resonances in Quantum Vacuum
- **Source:** casimirspace.com, Physical Review Research paper
- **Evidence Tier:** E1 (peer-reviewed theoretical claim)
- **Content:** White's model treats atomic orbitals as acoustic resonances in quantum fields around atoms. The average energy density around a hydrogen atom's ground state matches predictions for a Casimir cavity with separation equal to the Bohr radius.
- **Implication:** If correct, atoms ARE natural Casimir cavities. Engineering artificial Casimir cavities mimics what nature already does at atomic scale. This would provide theoretical grounding for energy extraction.
- **CRITICAL: This is the theoretical linchpin. If the dynamic vacuum model is correct, the entire program follows. If it's a mathematical reformulation rather than new physics (per critics), the engineering conclusions don't follow.**

### CLAIM-SW-006: Scaling Roadmap from Microwatts to Megawatts
- **Source:** JRE #2318, casimirspace.com
- **Evidence Tier:** E3 (commercial projections, unverified)
- **Content:**
  - **Tier 1 (2025-2026):** CA Microsparc — 5mm x 5mm, 1.5V, 25μA (~37.5μW). IoT sensors, key fobs, AirTags, medical monitors.
  - **Tier 2 (2027-2028):** Multi-layer die-stacking (16-24 layers), 100x power increase. ~3.4W board-level. Smartphones, AR glasses, EVs.
  - **Tier 3 (2030+):** 1kW modules. Homes, data centers, aircraft, ships.
- **Fabrication:** 2-week sprint cycles, conventional semiconductor nanofab, university partners (unnamed), MIT.nano partnership
- **CRITICAL: The leap from 37.5μW (Tier 1) to 1kW (Tier 3) is ~26 million times. Presented without technical substantiation for the scaling mechanism beyond "die stacking."**

### CLAIM-SW-007: DARPA ARRIVE Program Investigated Vacuum Engineering
- **Source:** DARPA website, multiple secondary sources
- **Evidence Tier:** E1 (government program, documented)
- **Content:** DARPA Defense Sciences Office ran the ARRIVE program (Applications Resulting from Recent Insights in Vacuum Engineering). Focused on engineering vacuum fluctuations in photonic and mechanical systems. Aimed to convert vacuum energy density into usable form.
- **Earlier program (~2009-2011):** Two-year, $10M Casimir Effect Enhancement initiative funding Harvard, Yale, UC Riverside, Argonne, Los Alamos.
- **Status:** ARRIVE listed as complete. No public reporting on whether milestones were met.
- **Significance:** DARPA invested real money in vacuum engineering. Program closure could indicate either transition to classified work or determination that approach didn't meet milestones.
