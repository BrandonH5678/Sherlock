### 6.5 Was This a Cohn Network Introduction?

**ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT:**

The hypothesis that Meister's introduction of Epstein to Wexner was orchestrated through the Cohn network requires evidence of:
1. Meister being connected to Cohn or Cohn's associates — **NO EVIDENCE FOUND**
2. Someone in the Cohn network directing Meister to make the introduction — **NO EVIDENCE FOUND**
3. Epstein being directed by Cohn network figures to target Wexner — **NO EVIDENCE FOUND**

What IS documented:
- Cohn represented Salerno (Genovese boss)
- Salerno was Walsh's co-conspirator
- Walsh was embedded in Wexner's operations
- Cohn died August 1986
- Epstein met Wexner ~1986
- The timing is suggestive

**Three competing hypotheses:**

**(A) Deliberate Cohn network placement (E3):** Cohn-adjacent operatives identified Wexner (already mob-penetrated through Walsh) as an ideal patron for Epstein and engineered the Meister introduction. This would require Meister being a witting or unwitting cutout. **No evidence supports this.**

**(B) Opportunistic exploitation of existing vulnerability (E2-E3):** Epstein (already networked through Bear Stearns/Greenberg) independently targeted Wexner because Wexner was known to be wealthy, poorly advised on finances, and already embedded in organized crime networks that would create leverage. The Meister introduction was genuine but Epstein's interest in Wexner was strategic. **Plausible but speculative.**

**(C) Coincidental networking (E2):** Insurance executive introduces financial guy to wealthy client on an airplane. Standard upper-class social dynamics, no conspiracy required. The mob connections to Wexner are background context, not causal factors. **Simplest explanation; fits known facts.**

**Confidence in deliberate Cohn-network orchestration: 0.15-0.25 (LOW)**
**Confidence in some form of network-aware targeting: 0.35-0.45 (LOW-MODERATE)**
**Confidence in coincidental introduction: 0.45-0.55 (MODERATE)**
