## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Roy Cohn represented bosses from exactly the three of five New York Mafia families most deeply embedded in the transatlantic heroin trade: **Genovese** (built the French Connection), **Bonanno** (operated the Pizza Connection that replaced it), and **Gambino** (ran the parallel Inzerillo-Sindona channel laundered through Vatican Bank/P2 infrastructure). His Lansky network connection through Lewis Rosenstiel provided the overarching financial architecture. This is not a coincidental client list — these three families controlled the American distribution end of a pipeline originating in CIA-protected territories, processed in CIA-protected labs, and laundered through Gladio-connected financial institutions.

**Key finding:** Cohn was the legal-political interface between the organized crime families that distributed heroin and the intelligence-political establishment that protected the supply chain. His role in the blackmail apparatus provided the coercive enforcement mechanism that kept political protectors in line.

## 1. THE PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 French Connection (1951-1972)

| Stage | Controller | Gladio/IC Connection |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Source:** Turkish opium farms | Licensed farmers, black market diversion | Turkish NATO member state |
| **Processing:** Marseille labs | Corsican syndicates (Guerini brothers, Unione Corse) | **CIA-protected** — Corsicans given dock control after CIA broke Communist strikes (1947, 1950). Tom Braden (CIA) admitted funding in 1967 editorial [E1] |
| **Transport:** Marseille → New York | Corsican shipping via CIA-controlled docks | CIA protection of Corsican dock operations |
| **Distribution:** New York | **Genovese family** (Luciano legacy) + **Bonanno family** (Montreal route) | Cohn represented both family bosses |
| **Laundering:** Offshore | Lansky network, Bahamas banking | Cohn connected through Rosenstiel |

**Scale:** By 1969, French Connection supplied **80% of America's heroin**. U.S. heroin addicts rose from 20,000 to 150,000 (1947-1967). [E1: Congressional Record, IPS report, May 7 1998]

**CIA complicity documented:** CIA joined forces with Corsican underworld to break Communist dock control in Marseille (1947, 1950). First heroin labs opened 1951, months after Corsicans took the waterfront. [E1: Tom Braden's own admission (1967); E1: McCoy, *The Politics of Heroin* (1972/1991), CIA attempted to suppress publication]

### 1.2 Pizza Connection (1975-1984) — Replaced French Connection

| Stage | Controller | Gladio/IC Connection |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Source:** Turkish/Southwest Asian opium | Same supply chain | Same NATO-protected territories |
| **Processing:** Sicilian refineries | Sicilian Mafia (French chemists "recycled" from Marseille) | Sicily: Mafia served as Gladio anti-communist paramilitary asset since 1943 |
| **Transport:** Sicily → New York | Bonanno family Sicilian pipeline | Formalized at 1957 Palermo Summit [E1: Buscetta testimony] |

**Scale:** $1.65 billion in heroin (1975-1984). [E1: Federal trial records, 18 convictions]

**Transition mechanics:** When French Connection collapsed (6 labs seized 1972-73, Turkey banned opium), the pipeline relocated to Sicily. French chemists were literally rehired by Sicilian refineries. "The Italian Connection took over from where the French Connection ended, but was much larger." [E1: Falcone investigation findings]

## 2. COHN'S CLIENTS IN THE NARCOTICS ARCHITECTURE

### 2.1 Genovese Family — Fat Tony Salerno (Cohn's client)

**Family's narcotics role:**
- **Vito Genovese:** The foundational CIA-Mafia-narcotics link. Served as translator for U.S. Army AMGOT in Naples post-invasion while simultaneously building a heroin empire. "The Hellowell Plan required Vito Genovese's return to the United States for the creation of a system of heroin distribution to the nightclubs of Harlem." [E1: Army CID records, Herlands Report; confirmed in Gladio audiobook timeline entries 748-1139]
- **Lucky Luciano:** Operation Underworld (1942-45) — Navy recruited Luciano for waterfront security and Sicily invasion intelligence. Deported 1946, built French Connection from Naples with Corsicans (1951). [E1: Navy records, Herlands Report]
- **Family controlled New York end** of French Connection through Luciano legacy

**Salerno's specific role:**
- Ran East Harlem drug and numbers rackets through 1960s
- "Dodged the heroin bullet" at boss level — maintained strategic distance from street trade
- Convicted in 1986 Commission Trial under RICO (covering narcotics trafficking among other charges) [E1: Federal trial records]
- **Cohn's legal work was remarkably effective:** arranged 6-month minimum security sentence for Salerno's gambling/tax evasion charges, got charges against 6 co-defendants dropped, enabled continued operations from inside [E1: Court records]
- Connected to Lansky's "clandestine investments and activities in the Bahamas" — same offshore ecosystem used for narcotics laundering [E2-E3]

**Assessment:** Salerno operated at a level of plausible deniability from the heroin trade itself, but the Genovese family's heroin infrastructure was its core revenue driver, and Salerno's RICO conviction included narcotics. Cohn's legal protection kept the boss-level insulation intact. **Confidence: 0.80 (E1-E2)**

### 2.2 Bonanno Family — Carmine Galante (Cohn's client)

**Galante: "The Godfather of Heroin"**

Galante was the most aggressive heroin trafficker among all American Mafia bosses. His career maps directly onto the Gladio-era pipeline:

1. **Montreal operations (1953-1956):** Organized smuggling of French Connection heroin from France through Canada to U.S. [E1: Canadian and U.S. law enforcement records]
2. **1957 Palermo Summit:** Attended the critical Grand Hotel des Palmes meeting (Oct 12-16, 1957) with Joe Bonanno, Lucky Luciano, Gaetano Badalamenti, and Tommaso Buscetta. **Agreement reached: Sicilian Mafia would smuggle heroin, Bonannos would distribute.** [E1: Buscetta testimony to Falcone; confirmed in Gladio audiobook timeline entries 213, 244, 1768, 2057, 2159, 2210, 2550]
3. **The "Zips":** Recruited Sicilian immigrants as bodyguards, enforcers, and heroin distributors — the operational backbone of Bonanno narcotics [E1: FBI surveillance, trial testimony]
4. **Assassinated (1979):** Commission-ordered hit because he was monopolizing the heroin trade [E1: NYPD records]

**Bonanno family's unique Sicilian advantage:** Joe Bonanno was born in Castellammare del Golfo, Sicily. The family maintained the most direct operational ties to Sicilian Cosa Nostra of any Five Families member — giving them the structural advantage that made the Pizza Connection possible.

**The Pizza Connection ($1.65B):** After Galante's death, the pipeline he built continued under Catalano and Badalamenti. Heroin processed in Sicily → distributed through pizza parlors → cash deposited in structured amounts under $10,000. [E1: Federal trial records, 17-month trial, 18 convictions]

**Cohn's role:** Represented the boss who built the entire Bonanno heroin infrastructure. Galante's operations directly connected to Sicilian Mafia networks that served as Gladio anti-communist assets. **Confidence: 0.90 (E1)**

### 2.3 Gambino Family — Paul Castellano + John Gotti (Both Cohn's clients)

**The Castellano Paradox:**
Castellano maintained Carlo Gambino's rule prohibiting family members from drug dealing (violators subject to death). Yet the Cherry Hill Gambinos operated a **$600M/year heroin pipeline** through the Inzerillo-Gambino-Spatola network. [E1: FBI wiretap evidence, trial records]

**Resolution:** Castellano's ban applied to the American faction; the Sicilian faction (Cherry Hill Gambinos) operated with significant autonomy. The heroin profits flowed through **Michele Sindona** — P2 member #0501, P2 Treasurer, who laundered Gambino heroin proceeds through Vatican Bank (IOR) and his Swiss bank Amincor since 1957. [E1: Pentito testimony, seized P2 membership list, Italian court records]

**This is the direct Gladio financial connection:** Gambino heroin money → Sindona → Vatican Bank → Swiss banks, flowing through the exact P2/Gladio financial infrastructure. Sindona was simultaneously:
- P2 Treasurer [E1: Seized membership list]
- Vatican Bank investment manager [E1: Financial records]
- Gambino family financial launderer [E1: Pentito testimony]

**John Gotti — Cohn enabled his rise:**
- Gotti was dealing heroin in violation of Castellano's ban
- Brother Gene Gotti and associate Angelo Ruggiero indicted for heroin dealing (1983) [E1: FBI wiretap recordings]
- Fear of Castellano's retribution was proximate justification for Castellano's murder (Dec 16, 1985, Sparks Steak House) [E1: FBI records, trial testimony]
- **Cohn's role was critical:** His negotiation of the McBratney murder plea (reduced to attempted manslaughter, 4yr sentence/served 2) enabled Gotti's rise to power. "John Gotti probably wouldn't have gone on to achieve such prominence" without Cohn's representation. [E2: Legal analysis, multiple Mafia historians]

**Assessment:** The Gambino family's heroin operations connected directly to Gladio financial infrastructure through Sindona. Cohn represented both the boss who maintained plausible deniability (Castellano) and the successor who was directly involved in heroin dealing (Gotti). **Confidence: 0.85 (E1-E2)**

## 3. THE SICILIAN MAFIA AS GLADIO ASSET — DOCUMENTED

### 3.1 Operation Husky (1943)

The Joint Chiefs of Staff approved a "Special Military Plan for Psychological Warfare in Sicily" (April 15, 1943) explicitly recommending "establishment of contact and communications with the leaders of... the Mafia." [E1: Declassified JCS document]

The Sicilian Mafia served as de facto anti-communist paramilitary:
- **Portella della Ginestra massacre (May 1, 1947):** 11 killed, 27 wounded at May Day celebration. Attributed to bandit Guiliano but widely believed ordered by higher powers (Mafia, landowners, politicians). Occurred simultaneously with CIA operations to prevent Communist electoral victories across Italy. [E1: Italian court records, Pisciotta testimony; E3 for direct CIA involvement]
- Violence against trade unionists and left-wing politicians in Sicily
- Control of rural territories preventing Communist organizing
- Collaboration with Christian Democratic politicians connected to Gladio structures [E2]

### 3.3 The Mafia's Dual Function

The Sicilian Mafia simultaneously served as:
1. **Gladio anti-communist asset** — suppressing leftist movements, providing paramilitary capability
2. **Heroin processing/transit hub** — refining and shipping heroin to American distribution networks

These were not separate operations. The same political protection that enabled the Mafia's anti-communist function shielded its narcotics operations. The same families (Bontade, Inzerillo, Badalamenti) that served as Gladio-useful anti-communist forces also ran the heroin refineries. [E2: Falcone investigations, Italian parliamentary commissions]
