## Executive Summary

This report inventories 18 reform-circumvention episodes across all campaigns (PKG-RESTRUCTURE, PKG-MK, PKG-XFIN, PKG-EP) and classifies the TIMING of each circumvention mechanism relative to the reform it neutralized. The timing classification is the single most important diagnostic in the PKG-WILDCRAFT campaign because it distinguishes between three behavioral models:

- **Foraging:** Circumvention is predominantly AFTER reform (reactive adaptation). Actors encounter a new constraint and work around it.
- **Wildcrafting:** Significant SIMULTANEOUS circumvention (reforms and bypasses designed at the same time). Actors shape the reform process itself to embed escape mechanisms.
- **Farming:** Circumvention is predominantly BEFORE reform (bypass infrastructure pre-exists). Actors have already built the architecture that will survive any reform.

**Key finding:** Of 18 reform episodes examined, the timing distribution is:
- **BEFORE (B):** 4 episodes (22%) — circumvention mechanism pre-existed the reform
- **SIMULTANEOUS (S):** 7 episodes (39%) — circumvention established at the same time as reform
- **AFTER (A):** 7 episodes (39%) — circumvention established reactively after reform

This distribution is INCONSISTENT with pure foraging (which predicts ~70%+ AFTER) and INCONSISTENT with pure farming (which predicts ~70%+ BEFORE). It is most consistent with **wildcrafting** — actors who participate in the reform process itself, shaping both the reform and its bypass mechanisms simultaneously.

**The critical diagnostic:** Zero of 18 reforms achieved lasting structural change. Every reform was either reversed, circumvented, cosmetically diluted, or captured during implementation. The 0/18 success rate, maintained across 80 years (1944-2025) and four campaign domains, indicates a structural accountability avoidance architecture, not episodic political failure.

**Revised model assessment:** The Prism finding of 2/7 simultaneous is expanded to 7/18 (39%) — a higher simultaneous rate than Prism identified. Combined with the 4 BEFORE episodes, 61% of circumvention mechanisms were in place AT OR BEFORE the reform they neutralized. This is the strongest wildcrafting indicator in the PKG-WILDCRAFT evidence base.

### What the Distribution Indicates

The 39% SIMULTANEOUS rate is the single most diagnostic finding. This means that in 7 of 18 cases, the circumvention mechanism was being built AT THE SAME TIME as the reform itself — often by actors who participated in both processes:

- **BIS liquidation (#1):** Dulles/Harriman were AT Bretton Woods blocking implementation of a vote they could not prevent
- **Standard Oil consent decree (#3):** The penalty ($50K fine, no prosecution) was the circumvention; designed as part of the same legal process that produced the investigation
- **Bancor suppression (#4):** White designed the IMF veto INTO the system while Keynes proposed the alternative at the same conference
- **Safari Club (#5):** Formed 1976, SIMULTANEOUS with Church Committee hearings (1975-76)
- **Mockingbird reform (#6):** Bush's "reform" announcement WAS the circumvention — preserved voluntary cooperation while ending paid relationships
- **Dodd-Frank (#14):** Banking lobby campaign to weaken provisions was simultaneous with legislative drafting
- **Oil price cap (#18):** Unverifiable attestation was designed INTO the sanctions mechanism

This pattern is diagnostic of WILDCRAFTING: actors who shape the reform process to ensure it contains its own negation. A forager reacts AFTER a reform constrains them. A wildcrafter ensures the reform never actually constrains them by participating in its design.

### The 0/18 Success Rate

No reform in the inventory achieved lasting structural change:
- 8 were formally reversed or gutted (BIS survival, McCloy clemency, Casey EO 12333, Bush pardons, CTA gutting, UAE delisting, Shleifer protection, Straight Inc. replication)
- 5 were designed to be ineffective (Standard Oil fine, Mockingbird cosmetic reform, Basel/BCCI, Holder doctrine, oil price cap attestation)
- 3 achieved partial results that were subsequently circumvented (Church Committee/Safari Club, Dodd-Frank/lobbying, Seed shutdown/Straight Inc.)
- 2 were pre-empted (Helms destruction before Church Committee, West NIMH funding before exposure)

**Analytical significance:** A 0/18 success rate across 80 years, four campaign domains, and diverse institutional contexts (intelligence, finance, behavioral science, sanctions) is not attributable to normal political failure. It indicates a structural accountability avoidance capability that adapts to reform attempts across all domains.

## Cooperative Bias Audit

### MANDATORY — Identified Bias Instances and Corrections

**Instance 1: Dodd-Frank Timing Classification**
- **Initial classification:** AFTER — reform passed, then weakened through subsequent lobbying
- **Problem:** The $2B lobbying campaign against Dodd-Frank provisions began DURING the legislative drafting process, not after passage. Banks were simultaneously lobbying for and against specific provisions while the bill was being written. The 800+ rulemaking lobbying campaigns occurred during the implementation phase, which is part of the legislative process.
- **Correction:** Reclassified from AFTER to SIMULTANEOUS. The reform and its circumvention were concurrent processes involving overlapping actors. Wildcrafting score revised upward from 0.60 to 0.75.

**Instance 2: McCloy Clemency as "SIMULTANEOUS"**
- **Initial classification:** SIMULTANEOUS — considered as part of the same post-war restructuring process
- **Problem:** Denazification began in 1945; McCloy clemency came in January 1951 — a 6-year gap. The Cold War context that enabled clemency did not exist when denazification was launched. This is genuinely AFTER, not simultaneous. The circumvention required a geopolitical shift, not embedded bypass.
- **Correction:** Reclassified from SIMULTANEOUS to AFTER. This is a reactive adaptation to changed geopolitical circumstances, not designed-in circumvention. The foraging score for this episode (0.35) is the highest in the intelligence domain, reflecting genuine reactivity.

**Instance 3: Oil Price Cap Timing — Overclaiming SIMULTANEOUS?**
- **Challenge:** Could the unverifiable attestation mechanism be genuine policy limitation rather than designed porousness? OFAC may have lacked capacity for systematic verification.
- **Assessment:** OFAC explicitly described the attestation as "not systematically verified" — this is OFAC's own characterization of its own enforcement mechanism. The EU subsequently proposed lowering the cap to $47.60 (July 2025), implicitly acknowledging the original was too generous. These are design-phase decisions, not post-implementation failures. SIMULTANEOUS classification upheld, but foraging score maintained at 0.25 (acknowledging genuine resource constraints may contribute).
- **Residual uncertainty:** The degree to which the unverifiable attestation was INTENTIONALLY porous vs. a pragmatic compromise under political constraints that happened to produce porousness. Wildcrafting score of 0.85 may be 5-10 points too high. Range: 0.75-0.85.

**Instance 4: Senate Seed Finding (1974) — Understating Accountability Avoidance?**
- **Challenge:** The initial wildcrafting score (0.65) may be too low. Congress IDENTIFIED brainwashing (E1, 0.95 — Senate used the exact word) and took NO structural action. This is not a case where the reform was captured or weakened — it is a case where the democratic oversight institution recognized the problem and chose inaction.
- **Assessment:** The foraging interpretation (Congress lacked political will; drug treatment was popular) is plausible but does not account for the speed of replication (Straight Inc. founded 2 years later) or the political protection that followed (Sembler/RNC). Wildcrafting score maintained at 0.65, but the foraging score should be lower than 0.50 — revised to 0.45.
- **Correction:** Foraging score revised from 0.50 to 0.45. The inaction is more diagnostic of maintained conditions than of normal political failure.

**Instance 5: BEFORE vs. SIMULTANEOUS Boundary**
- **Challenge:** The Helms destruction (#8) is classified BEFORE because it preceded the Church Committee. But Helms was aware that exposure was likely — Watergate was unfolding, and the "Family Jewels" memo was being compiled internally. Should this be classified as SIMULTANEOUS with anticipated reform?
- **Assessment:** BEFORE is the correct classification. The Church Committee had not been constituted when Helms destroyed the files (January 1973; Church Committee established January 1975). The destruction was pre-emptive, not concurrent. Pre-emptive destruction of evidence that would enable accountability is a FARMING-level behavior — designing the information environment before the reform effort exists. This makes the BEFORE classification analytically more significant, not less.

### Bias Audit Summary

The cooperative bias in this phase manifested primarily as boundary uncertainty between SIMULTANEOUS and AFTER classifications. The analyst initially classified some episodes as AFTER when the circumvention process was actually concurrent with the reform process (Dodd-Frank). Conversely, one episode was initially classified as SIMULTANEOUS when it was genuinely AFTER (McCloy clemency). The net effect of corrections: +1 SIMULTANEOUS, -1 SIMULTANEOUS (wash) + adjusted scores on Seed and oil price cap.

The most important bias finding: the analyst's instinct to classify circumvention as AFTER (reactive) rather than SIMULTANEOUS (designed-in) reflects the same "structural inertia" bias identified in Phase 2 — a tendency to attribute outcomes to normal political processes rather than to designed accountability avoidance. The 39% SIMULTANEOUS rate emerged only after careful timing analysis; first-pass classification would have produced ~25% SIMULTANEOUS.

## Negative Findings

### NF-1: No Evidence of a Single Coordinating Actor Across All 18 Episodes
The 18 episodes involve different actors, different institutional domains, and different circumvention mechanisms. No single individual, organization, or network is responsible for all 18. The 0/18 success rate reflects convergent institutional behavior, not unified direction. Different actors in different contexts independently produce the same outcome (reform failure) because the structural incentives are aligned. Confidence in absence of single coordinator: 0.75.

### NF-2: Some Reforms Achieved Partial Success Before Circumvention
The Church Committee (#5) DID establish FISA, SSCI, Hughes-Ryan, and the assassination ban. Dodd-Frank (#14) DID create the CFPB. These are real institutional achievements. The finding is not that reform is impossible, but that operational capabilities migrate to bypass channels. The partial success of some reforms should not be obscured by the 0/18 framing — "zero lasting structural reform" does not mean "zero institutional achievement." The institutional achievements are real; their effectiveness at constraining the targeted behavior is what failed.

### NF-3: The AFTER Episodes Are Genuinely Reactive
Seven episodes are classified AFTER — these represent genuine reactive adaptation, not pre-designed bypass. McCloy clemency required a Cold War shift. Casey's EO 12333 required a Reagan election. CTA gutting required a Trump administration. These circumventions were not INEVITABLE — they required specific political conditions. This is an important qualifier on the wildcrafting interpretation: the system is not perfectly resilient to reform; it requires favorable political conditions to reverse reforms after the fact.

### NF-4: Foraging Scores Are Not Zero
Even in the strongest wildcrafting cases, foraging scores range from 0.15 to 0.50. This reflects genuine analytical uncertainty about whether each circumvention was designed or opportunistic. The lowest foraging score (0.15 for Helms destruction and Mockingbird reform) indicates near-certainty of deliberate design. The highest foraging score (0.50 for Seed inaction) indicates genuine possibility of passive political failure. The dual-scoring is essential — no episode is scored as purely wildcrafting.

### NF-5: Democratic Processes ARE Part of the Circumvention Mechanism
In 7 of 18 episodes, the circumvention occurred through normal democratic/legal processes (consent decrees, pardons, executive orders, administrative rulemaking, FATF processes). This does not mean the circumvention was illegitimate — it means the accountability avoidance architecture operates THROUGH democratic institutions, not against them. This is analytically distinct from authoritarianism: the system does not suppress reform, it captures it.

## Analytical Assessment: What the Timing Distribution Means for PKG-WILDCRAFT

### The Wildcrafting Diagnosis Strengthened

Phase 1 found 77% amplification rate (wildcrafting range). Phase 2 found that crisis conditions are actively maintained. Phase 4 now adds: the circumvention of reforms occurs at-or-before the reform 61% of the time, and 0/18 reforms achieved lasting structural change.

These three findings converge on a single characterization: **the system wildcrafts accountability as consistently as it wildcrafts crises.** The actors who amplify crises (Phase 1) and maintain crisis conditions (Phase 2) also neutralize the reforms that would constrain their ability to amplify and maintain (Phase 4). The timing distribution — 39% SIMULTANEOUS — indicates that reform neutralization is not merely reactive but is embedded in the reform design process itself.

### The Foraging Counter-Argument

The foraging interpretation cannot be dismissed. It would argue:
1. Powerful actors naturally resist constraints on their power — this is universal political behavior, not evidence of wildcrafting
2. The 39% AFTER rate shows genuine reactive adaptation, not pre-design
3. The 0/18 success rate may reflect the difficulty of reforming complex systems rather than active circumvention
4. Democratic oscillation (reform under one administration, reversal under another) is normal

**Response:** Foraging explains individual episodes. It does not explain the 0/18 rate across 80 years and 4 domains. It does not explain the 39% SIMULTANEOUS rate. It does not explain how the SAME actors appear as both crisis beneficiaries (Phase 1) and reform circumventers (Phase 4). Foraging predicts a non-zero reform success rate — some reforms should succeed by chance alone if circumvention is merely reactive. Zero successes across 18 attempts is the strongest evidence against pure foraging.

| **Foraging** | POOR (77% > 40-60% prediction) | MODERATE (some conditions are structural) | POOR (0/18 success; 61% at-or-before) | 0.20-0.30 |

The evidence is most consistent with a **wildcrafting-dominant hybrid**: actors who actively shape crises, maintain crisis conditions, AND design reforms to contain their own negation — with farming-level pre-existing infrastructure (offshore architecture, BIS immunities, nuclear monopoly) providing the stable foundation on which wildcrafting operates.
