## SECTION 1: SUBJECT IDENTIFICATION

### 1.1 The Three Rothschild Financial Entities — Critical Distinctions

The "Rothschild" financial world in the relevant period comprises three principal institutional entities. Conflating them is a significant analytical error common in open-source reporting.

**Entity A: Edmond de Rothschild Group (EdR)**
- **Origin:** Founded 1953 by Edmond Adolphe de Rothschild (French branch) as La Compagnie Financière (LCF) Edmond de Rothschild, Paris
- **Private banking arm:** Banque Privée Edmond de Rothschild, Geneva, established 1965
- **Luxembourg branch:** 1968
- **Legal structure:** 100% family-owned (private) as of March 2019
- **Headquarters:** Geneva, Switzerland; also Paris, Luxembourg, Monaco
- **AUM (2024):** CHF 184 billion (~$204 billion) — reported 12% growth year-over-year
- **Employees:** ~2,700 across 28 offices in 14 countries
- **Leadership post-2021:** Ariane de Rothschild (CEO from March 2023; first non-Rothschild bloodline to lead a Rothschild institution)
- **Leadership 1997–2021:** Benjamin de Rothschild (died January 15, 2021, cardiac arrest, age 57, Pregny-Chambésy, Switzerland)
- **Primary focus:** Private banking, wealth management, alternative assets, fund of funds (pioneered this model in 1969)
- **AUM as of early 2026:** CHF 200+ billion (record level per bank statement February 2026)
- **EPSTEIN DIRECT CONNECTION:** Yes — this is the entity named in WEX-060. The $25M agreement (October 5, 2015) was between Epstein's Southern Trust Company, Inc. and **Edmond de Rothschild Holding S.A.**, signed by Ariane de Rothschild.

**Entity B: Rothschild & Co (R&Co)**
- **Origin:** Merger of English N.M. Rothschild & Sons (founded 1798 by Nathan Mayer Rothschild) and French Rothschild & Cie Banque, formalized 2003 under David René de Rothschild; renamed from Paris Orléans to Rothschild & Co
- **Headquarters:** Paris and London
- **Structure:** Listed company (Euronext Paris)
- **Business lines:** (1) Global Advisory (M&A, restructuring — top-10 globally by deal count); (2) Wealth and Asset Management (€124B AUM, end 2024); (3) Five Arrows (alternative assets)
- **Asset Management division (Rothschild & Co Asset Management):** ~€40B AUM, 180+ employees, 9 European countries, institutional/intermediary focus; also registered in US (Rothschild & Co Asset Management US Inc., SEC-registered)
- **EPSTEIN DIRECT CONNECTION:** Not established by primary sources. No documented contract, agreement, or business relationship found between Epstein and R&Co specifically.
- **NOTE:** Epstein's February 2016 email to Peter Thiel described a bank with "$160 billion under management" — which matches EdR's approximate 2016 AUM range, not R&Co's €124B figure. This supports the interpretation that Epstein was referring to the EdR entity.

**Entity C: RIT Capital Partners (Jacob Rothschild)**
- Lord Jacob Rothschild (English branch, 4th Baron Rothschild) led RIT Capital Partners, a listed investment trust
- Evelyn de Rothschild (knighted, died November 2022) was a separate figure, also English branch
- **EPSTEIN CONNECTION:** Evelyn de Rothschild and Edouard de Rothschild both appear in Epstein's FBI-seized "Little Black Book" (2005) with London and Paris addresses respectively — contact-level association only

### 1.2 The Name Dispute (Resolved 2018)

After Paris Orléans was renamed to Rothschild & Co, the EdR Group disputed the use of the family name for branding purposes. Until their 2018 settlement, the two branches were cross-shareholders in each other's businesses. The cross-shareholding and name dispute resolution are important context: in 2015, when Epstein signed his contract with EdR, the two branches were still technically cross-shareholders — the institutional boundaries were somewhat porous.
