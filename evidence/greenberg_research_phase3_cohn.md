## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Alan "Ace" Greenberg and Roy Cohn were contemporaneous figures in the uppermost tier of New York elite society from at least the late 1970s through Cohn's death on August 2, 1986. Their documented connection crystallized at the May 2, 1983 B'nai B'rith Banking and Finance Lodge State of Israel Bond dinner at the Grand Hyatt Hotel, where Greenberg served as an honorary co-chairman alongside Donald Trump and Rupert Murdoch in an event honoring Cohn. This was not a chance encounter: Cohn was himself a member of the Banking and Finance Lodge of B'nai B'rith, and Greenberg's role as co-chairman — specifically in the context of an event honoring Cohn — indicates a pre-existing social relationship of sufficient warmth to commit his name and institutional standing to public honor.

The significance of the Greenberg-Cohn connection extends beyond biography. Cohn's operational portfolio — blackmail operations, organized crime legal work, CIA connections through William Casey, media control through Rupert Murdoch and Si Newhouse — overlaps at multiple points with the institutional architecture that later housed Jeffrey Epstein. Greenberg hired Epstein (1976), maintained the relationship (1981–2008), and in 1983 publicly co-honored the man whose network most scholars identify as the structural predecessor to Epstein's own operation. The simultaneity of these connections is the central analytical finding of Phase 3.

**Key finding:** Greenberg appears in the B'nai B'rith event documentation as "then-head of Bear Stearns." Cohn was specifically a member of the Banking and Finance Lodge that honored him. This was an insider event for the New York Jewish financial and legal establishment — not general-public philanthropy. Greenberg's presence as co-chairman signals active membership in Cohn's social world, not peripheral acquaintance.
