## 2. GLADIO'S US COMPONENT — WHAT IS ACTUALLY DOCUMENTED

### 2.1 The Allied Clandestine Committee and "United States" Listing

The most direct evidence of a US domestic Gladio component is the Belgian Parliamentary
Committee's finding that the Allied Clandestine Committee coordinated stay-behind networks
including one in the United States. This is the single most important piece of evidence
for a US domestic structure.

| US Gladio Evidence | Detail | Evidence Tier |
|-------------------|--------|---------------|
| Belgian parliamentary finding | ACC "responsible for coordinating the 'Stay-behind' networks in... the United States" | E1 (Belgian Parliamentary Committee on Gladio) |
| US State Department 2006 | Published a communique confirming existence of NATO stay-behind efforts generally and the Gladio unit in Italy specifically | E1 (State Department public communique, 2006) |
| CIA's role in creating networks | The CIA's foundational role in creating and funding European stay-behind networks is E1-confirmed via Church Committee | E1 (Church Committee) |
| Formal US domestic network | Whether a formal US domestic stay-behind network equivalent to European Gladio networks existed is **contested and unconfirmed** | E2-E3 |
| Congressional investigation of domestic stay-behind | No specific congressional investigation of a US domestic stay-behind network has been identified in public records | Negative finding |
| Declassified documents naming US Gladio structure | **No declassified US government document naming a domestic Gladio-equivalent network has been publicly identified** | Negative finding |

**Analyst assessment:** The Belgian parliamentary finding is the key E1 data point. It
establishes that, within NATO's coordination structure, the United States was listed
alongside European nations as having an ACC-coordinated stay-behind network. However:
(1) this is a Belgian finding about NATO structure, not a US admission or US document;
(2) the reference to "United States" may describe the US role in coordinating the overall
network rather than a domestic US stay-behind unit; and (3) the content, membership, and
operations of any such domestic US structure remain undisclosed in public records.

The logical inference that the US, as the primary architect of European stay-behind
networks, would have a domestic equivalent is structurally reasonable — but inference
is not documentation.

### 5.2 The COINTELPRO/CHAOS vs. Gladio Distinction

This distinction is analytically critical. Gladio in Italy:
- Involved mass-casualty attacks on civilian targets (strategy of tension)
- Was run through neofascist operational groups
- Had CIA financial support confirmed at parliamentary inquiry level
- Used Masonic coordination structures with intelligence penetration

COINTELPRO/CHAOS:
- Involved surveillance, harassment, disinformation, and illegal mail opening
- Were FBI/CIA programs directed from within official agencies
- Did not involve mass-casualty attacks on US soil
- Were investigated and confirmed by Church Committee

These are related in anti-communist ideology but structurally distinct. Describing
COINTELPRO as "US Gladio" collapses this distinction and overstates the evidence.

## APPENDIX: ANALYST'S HONEST SUMMARY

A researcher approaching the DeBartolo-Gladio hypothesis should understand three things:

**1. The European Gladio record is robust; the US domestic record is sparse.**
Italy has parliamentary inquiry findings, court convictions (Bologna massacre), named
participants, and specific operational details. The US domestic equivalent, even if it
existed, has not been subjected to equivalent disclosure.

**2. DeBartolo's organized crime profile is real and documented; his Gladio profile
is inferential and undocumented.**
The organized crime connections are graded E2 across multiple independent government
sources. The Gladio connections are graded E3 — structural inference based on the
European pattern, not documented operational relationships.

**3. Proximity to CIA-connected organized crime figures is not equivalent to Gladio
participation.**
