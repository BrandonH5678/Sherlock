## The Analytical Gap This Campaign Fills

Previous Sherlock analysis used a binary: **engineered** (rejected at 0.15) vs. **exploited** (confirmed at 0.80). This binary obscures a critical middle category.

### Four Models to Test

**Model A: FORAGING (Pure Exploitation)** — Current Sherlock default (0.45-0.55)
- Crises arise from genuine systemic dynamics. Positioned actors exploit them opportunistically.
- Prediction: Amplification/stabilization decisions should be MIXED. Some post-crisis reforms should succeed.

**Model B: WILDCRAFTING (Cultivation)** — The new hypothesis to score
- Actors don't create crises but TEND the conditions that produce them through 5 mechanisms:
  1. Environmental maintenance (keeping structural instability conditions active)
  2. Amplification at critical phase (feeding nascent crises rather than stabilizing)
  3. Actor breeding (credentialing pipelines selecting for crisis-compatible traits)
  4. Anti-fragile positioning (building systems that profit from ANY crisis)
  5. Accountability avoidance (capturing post-crisis reforms before next cycle)
- Prediction: SYSTEMATIC amplification (>75%). ALL reforms circumvented. Environmental conditions maintained when reform is feasible.

**Model C: FARMING (Full Engineering)** — Currently at 0.15
- Crises are engineered from inception via false flags, manufactured triggers, coordinated destabilization.
- Prediction: Direct E1 evidence of trigger creation traceable to benefiting actors.

**Model D: BREEDING (Actor Cultivation)** — Extension of wildcrafting
- Credentialing pipelines (S&B, KA, siloviki, princelings, seminaries) select for psychopathic traits, producing leaders who naturally tend crisis conditions as behavioral output.
- Prediction: Pipeline graduates show measurably different behavioral profiles than non-pipeline leaders.

### Phase 4: Accountability Avoidance Chronology
**Input:** All reform-circumvention episodes across ALL campaigns (not just PKG-RESTRUCTURE)
**Output:** Comprehensive timeline with:
- Reform episode identified
- Circumvention mechanism
- PRECISE TIMING: Was circumvention established BEFORE the reform, SIMULTANEOUSLY, or AFTER?
  - BEFORE = farming indicator (pre-planned)
  - SIMULTANEOUS = wildcrafting indicator (proactive tending)
  - AFTER = could be foraging (reactive) or wildcrafting (next-cycle preparation)
- Was reform circumvention successful? (Yes/No)
- Did the condition that generated the original crisis persist after circumvention?

**This timing analysis is the single most important diagnostic in the campaign.** Under foraging, timing should be predominantly reactive (AFTER). Under wildcrafting, timing should show significant SIMULTANEOUS or BEFORE patterns. Under farming, timing should be predominantly BEFORE.

**Prism preliminary analysis found:** 2/7 simultaneous, 3/7 within-cycle, 2/7 progressive capture. Zero successful reforms. Expand this analysis to ALL campaigns.
